# Croatian Grammar Reference — Ground Truth

Authoritative tables for CroLearn. **Every Croatian form in any lesson, story, test, or note must match these tables.** Content generation prompts must include the relevant sections. Standard Croatian, ijekavian.

## 1. Alphabet & pronunciation (30 letters)

a b c č ć d dž đ e f g h i j k l lj m n nj o p r s š t u v z ž

| Letter | Sound (approx.) | | Letter | Sound (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| c | ts in "cats" | | lj | lli in "million" |
| č | hard ch ("church") | | nj | ny in "canyon" |
| ć | soft ch (t + y-ish) | | š | sh |
| dž | hard j ("judge") | | ž | s in "measure" |
| đ | soft j (d + y-ish) | | j | y in "yes" |
| h | rough h ("loch") | | r | rolled; can be syllabic (prst, Hrvatska) |

Golden rule: **one letter = one sound, always. Read exactly as written.** Stress: never on the last syllable; most often the first.

## 2. Noun gender (NOM sg endings)

| Gender | Typical ending | Examples | Traps |
|---|---|---|---|
| masculine | consonant | grad, stol, čovjek | a few -a nouns are masc (tata, kolega); -o names (Marko) |
| feminine | -a | žena, kava, škola | consonant-final fem -i type: noć, stvar, ljubav, sol |
| neuter | -o / -e | selo, pivo, more, dijete | -me type: ime → imena; vrijeme → vremena |

## 3. Noun declension — the core tables

### Masculine (inanimate: grad · animate: student)

| Case | sg inanim | sg anim | pl |
|---|---|---|---|
| NOM | grad | student | gradovi / studenti |
| GEN | grada | studenta | gradova / studenata |
| DAT | gradu | studentu | gradovima / studentima |
| ACC | **grad (=NOM)** | **studenta (=GEN)** | gradove / studente |
| VOC | grade | studente | gradovi / studenti |
| LOC | gradu | studentu | gradovima / studentima |
| INS | gradom | studentom | gradovima / studentima |

- **Animate ACC sg = GEN; inanimate ACC sg = NOM.** (Plural ACC is -e for both.)
- Long plural: most monosyllables insert -ov-/-ev-: grad→gradovi, muž→muževi (after č ć đ š ž j lj nj → -ev-, and INS sg -em: mužem).
- Fleeting a: pas→psa, momak→momka, vrabac→vrapca; reappears in GEN pl: studenata, momaka.

### Feminine -a (žena) and feminine -i (noć)

| Case | žena sg | žene pl | noć sg | noći pl |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NOM | žena | žene | noć | noći |
| GEN | žene | žena | noći | noći |
| DAT | ženi | ženama | noći | noćima |
| ACC | ženu | žene | noć | noći |
| VOC | ženo | žene | noći | noći |
| LOC | ženi | ženama | noći | noćima |
| INS | ženom | ženama | noći/noću | noćima |

- GEN pl of -a nouns: bare -a, with inserted -a- in clusters: sestra→sestara, mačka→mačaka; some take -i: majki/majka.
- DAT/LOC sg sibilarization: ruka→ruci, noga→nozi, knjiga→knjizi (k→c, g→z, h→s).

### Neuter (selo, more; ime-type)

| Case | selo sg | sela pl | more sg | ime sg | ime pl |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NOM/ACC/VOC | selo | sela | more | ime | imena |
| GEN | sela | sela | mora | imena | imena |
| DAT/LOC | selu | selima | moru | imenu | imenima |
| INS | selom | selima | morem | imenom | imenima |

- dijete → GEN djeteta; plural is suppletive: **djeca** (declines like fem sg: GEN djece, DAT djeci).

## 4. Adjective declension (nov "new")

| Case | m sg | f sg | n sg | m pl | f pl | n pl |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NOM | nov(i) | nova | novo | novi | nove | nova |
| GEN | novog(a) | nove | novog(a) | novih | novih | novih |
| DAT | novom(u) | novoj | novom(u) | novim(a) | novim(a) | novim(a) |
| ACC | nov / novog(a)* | novu | novo | nove | nove | nova |
| LOC | novom(e) | novoj | novom(e) | novim(a) | novim(a) | novim(a) |
| INS | novim | novom | novim | novim(a) | novim(a) | novim(a) |

\* ACC m sg follows the noun: inanimate = NOM, animate = GEN. Bracketed vowels are optional long forms.

Comparative: -iji (star→stariji, nov→noviji) or -ši/-i with mutation (jak→jači, skup→skuplji, mlad→mlađi). Irregular: dobar→**bolji**, loš/zao→**gori**, velik→**veći**, malen/mali→**manji**, dug→dulji/duži. Superlative: naj- + comparative (najbolji). Comparison: **od + GEN** or **nego + NOM**.

## 7. Present tense — three classes

| | -am (gledati) | -im (voljeti) | -em (piti) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ja | gledam | volim | pijem |
| ti | gledaš | voliš | piješ |
| on/a | gleda | voli | pije |
| mi | gledamo | volimo | pijemo |
| vi | gledate | volite | pijete |
| oni | gledaju | **vole** | **piju** |

The infinitive doesn't predict the class — learn present 1sg with each verb. Frequent irregulars: ići→idem, jesti→jedem, piti→pijem, zvati (se)→zovem, spavati→spavam, pisati→pišem, kupovati→kupujem, putovati→putujem.

## 8. Perfekt (past tense)

**clitic of biti + l-participle.** Participle: infinitive stem + -o/-la/-lo (sg), -li/-le/-la (pl), agreeing with the subject:

- raditi → radio / radila / radilo / radili / radile / radila
- Irregular participles: ići→išao/išla; moći→mogao/mogla; jesti→jeo/jela; htjeti→htio/htjela; doći→došao/došla; reći→rekao/rekla.
- Negation: nisam radio. Question: Jesi li radio? / Je li radila?
- 3sg + se: **je drops** → Zvao se Marko (not ~~Zvao se je~~).

## 9. Futur I and Futur II

- **Futur I**: infinitive + htjeti clitic. Clitic after verb: **pisat ću** (-ti drops the i in spelling), **doći ću** (-ći keeps). Clitic earlier: Ja ću pisati. / Sutra ću doći. Negative: neću pisati. Question: Hoćeš li doći?
- **Futur II** (in kad/ako/čim clauses): budem, budeš, bude, budemo, budete, budu + l-participle: Kad budem imao vremena…

## 10. Imperative & conditional

- Imperative: 2sg -j/-i (čitaj!, radi!, pij!), 1pl -jmo/-imo, 2pl -jte/-ite. Irregular: budi (biti), jedi (jesti), dođi (doći). Negative: **nemoj / nemojmo / nemojte + infinitive** (or ne + imperfective imperative).
- Conditional I: **bih, bi, bi, bismo, biste, bi** + l-participle: Htio bih kavu. Kad bih imao vremena, došao bih.
- Conditional II: bio/bila bih + l-participle (B2).

## 11. Verb aspect

Almost every verb comes in a pair: **imperfective** (process/habit) ↔ **perfective** (completed event, no true present meaning).

| Imperfective | Perfective | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| pisati | napisati | prefix na- |
| čitati | pročitati | prefix pro- |
| raditi | uraditi/napraviti | prefix / suppletive |
| kupovati | kupiti | stem change |
| davati | dati | stem change |
| govoriti | reći | suppletive |
| gledati | pogledati | prefix po- |
| počinjati | početi | stem change |

Usage anchors: present tense of a perfective ≠ "now" (used in future/subordinate contexts) · past: Pisao sam pismo (was writing) vs Napisao sam pismo (finished it) · future: Pisat ću (will be writing/will write regularly) vs Napisat ću (will get it written).

### Pair-formation patterns (B1 U17)

1. **Prefixation** — pf = prefix + impf, core meaning unchanged; the prefix choice is lexical (learn per verb): na- (pisati→napisati, učiti→naučiti, praviti→napraviti), pro- (čitati→pročitati), po- (gledati→pogledati, slušati→poslušati, piti→popiti), po-+stem (jesti→pojesti).
2. **-nu- perfectives** — pf in -nuti: dizati→dignuti (dignem; participle **digao, digla** — standard drops -nu; dignuo also heard), kretati→krenuti (krenem; krenuo, krenula — keeps -nu).
3. **Suffix / stem change** — the impf half typically carries -ava-/-iva-/-a- or a longer stem: kupovati→kupiti · davati→dati (dam; dao) · počinjati→početi (počnem; počeo) · završavati→završiti · objavljivati (objavljujem!)→objaviti · uzimati→uzeti (uzmem; uzeo) · prenositi→prenijeti (prenesem; **prenio, prenijela**). Reading trick: meet a perfective, add -avati/-ivati and you usually have its imperfective.
4. **Suppletive** — unrelated stems: govoriti→**reći** (rekao, rekla; fut. reći ću; the everyday present "I say" is **kažem**, from kazati) · dolaziti→**doći** (dođem; došao, došla) · odlaziti→**otići** (odem; otišao, otišla).

Other anchors: dogoditi se (pf; Što se dogodilo? — se, no je needed beyond the dropped se+je rule: Što se dogodilo, Dogodilo se…) · **habits are imperfective** even when each act completes (Svaki dan kupujem novine) · **narration spine**: dok + imperfective sets the background, perfective chain carries the events (Dok je Ana gledala vijesti, Marko je došao.) — the dok-clause verb and the main-clause perfective are the B1 U17 L4 target.

## 12. Clitic order (the second-position cluster)

Clitics live in **second position** in the clause, in FIXED internal order:

```
 li  →  aux (sam si smo ste su · ću ćeš će ćemo ćete · bih bi bismo biste)  →  DAT  →  ACC/GEN  →  se  →  je
```

- Dao **sam mu ga**. (aux → DAT → ACC)
- Jesi **li mi ga** dao? (li is in the verb jesi… actually: Jesi li mi ga dao?)
- **Je** goes LAST: Dala **mu ga je**.
- se + je → je usually drops: Nasmijala **se** (not ~~se je~~).

## 13. Numbers & case government

| Number | Governs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (jedan/jedna/jedno) | agrees like an adjective | jedan grad, jedna žena |
| 2 (dva m/n, dvije f), 3, 4 | **GEN sg** (paucal) | dva grada, dvije žene, tri piva |
| 5+ (and 11–20) | **GEN pl** | pet gradova, šest žena, jedanaest studenata |
| 21, 22–24… | by last digit | dvadeset jedan grad, dvadeset dva grada |

Tens: trideset, četrdeset, pedeset, šezdeset, sedamdeset, osamdeset, devedeset, sto. Ordinals: prvi, drugi, treći, četvrti, peti…

## 14. Prepositions by case

| Case | Prepositions |
|---|---|
| GEN | iz (from/out of), od (from), do (to/until), kod (at sb's), bez (without), blizu, pored, ispred, iza, iznad, ispod, prije, poslije, tijekom |
| DAT | k/ka (towards), prema (towards), unatoč (despite) |
| ACC | u/na (**direction**), kroz (through), za (for), uz (along/with) |
| LOC | u/na (**location**), o (about), po (around/according to), pri |
| INS | s/sa (with, company), među, nad, pod, pred, za (position variants) |

**The u/na rule pair:** motion → ACC (Idem **u grad**, **na fakultet**); location → LOC (Živim **u gradu**, radim **na fakultetu**). "na-words" (islands, events, open spaces, institutions): na otok/otoku, na posao/poslu, na koncert(u), na fakultet(u), na trg(u).

**INS s/sa:** with company/accompaniment (kava **s mlijekom**, idem **s Anom**); means of transport takes BARE INS (putujem **vlakom**, idem **autom** — no s!).

## 15. Question formation

- Yes/no: **Je li…? / Jesi li…?** (verb + li), colloquial **Da li…** (avoid in course — mark as heard-but-not-taught)
- Wh-words: tko (who), što (what), gdje (where/loc), kamo (where to/dir), kada (when), zašto (why), kako (how), koliko (how much), koji (which), čiji (whose)
- tko/što decline: tko, koga, komu, koga, kome, kim · što, čega, čemu, što, čemu, čime
