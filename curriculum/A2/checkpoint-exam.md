# A2 Checkpoint Exam — Specification

The level-exit exam for A2 (after Unit 16), per [../01-teaching-method.md](../01-teaching-method.md) §8. Four skills — listening, reading, grammar/vocab cloze, speaking — **plus the guided writing task** that A2+ adds. Everything from Units 1–16 is fair game; the A2 spine (future I, DAT, modals, INS, GEN pl, comparatives, VOC, imperative, clitic pronouns, aspect intro) carries the weight.

## Format at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Items | **45** (40–60 band): listening 10 · reading 10 · cloze 20 · speaking 4 prompts · writing 1 task |
| Time | ~45 min (soft guidance per section, no hard timer per item) |
| Pass | ≥ 70% of total weighted points |
| Gate | Soft (per §8): warn on fail, allow proceed; every wrong item becomes a priority SRS card; wrong items also feed the AI exercise generator |
| Scoring | First attempt only, no hints, no retry queue; diacritic-tolerant grading on typed answers ("correct, mind the diacritic") |

**Point weights:** listening 10 pts · reading 10 pts · cloze 20 pts · speaking 4×5 = 20 pts (rubric) · writing 15 pts (rubric) = **75 pts total; pass ≥ 53**.

## Coverage map (what must be sampled)

Every A2 spine item appears at least twice across the exam, in at least two different sections:

| Spine item | Introduced | Must appear in |
|---|---|---|
| Future I (incl. pisat ću spelling) | U9 | cloze, speaking |
| DAT sg + sviđati se | U10 | cloze, listening |
| Modals + infinitive | U11 | cloze, reading |
| INS (s/sa company; bare means) | U12 | cloze, listening |
| GEN pl + numbers/quantities | U13 | cloze, listening, writing |
| Comparatives/superlatives, od/nego | U13 | cloze, reading, speaking |
| VOC | U14 | reading (invitation), cloze |
| Imperative + nemoj | U14 | cloze, listening, writing |
| Clitic pronouns + cluster order | U15 | cloze, reading, speaking |
| Aspect (past + future choice) | U16 | cloze, reading, speaking |
| A1 base (cases NOM–GEN sg, present, perfekt) | U1–8 | everywhere, unmarked |

## Section 1 — Listening (10 items, ~10 min)

TTS audio; each item playable twice at normal speed, once slow.

- **Passage A (4 items):** a 60–80-word dialogue — Ana phones Tom to invite him to a celebration (dates, imperative, clitics). 4 MC comprehension questions answerable only by understanding (per §7 rule).
- **Passage B (3 items):** a 50–70-word monologue — Marko describes his summer plans (future I, aspect, comparatives). 3 MC.
- **Dictation (3 items):** listen-type single sentences, e.g. *Javi mi se sutra!* · *Na plaži ima puno ljudi.* · *Putujem vlakom u Split.*

Example MC: TTS says *»Proslava je petog lipnja, nemoj zakasniti!«* — Q: "Kada je proslava?" (5.6. / 15.6. / 5.7.)

## Section 2 — Reading (10 items, ~10 min)

- **Passage A (5 items):** a 180–220-word story-register text with the recurring cast (Tom's year in review: past narration with aspect contrast, a trip, a celebration), ≤3 glossed words. 5 MC comprehension.
- **Passage B (3 items):** a pozivnica + a short formal e-mail reply (VOC salutations, dates in GEN, imperative). 3 MC (kada, gdje, tko dolazi).
- **Micro-items (2):** 2 standalone MC — pick the sentence that matches a described situation (aspect choice: *Pisao je pismo* vs *Napisao je pismo*; clitic order: which of three orders is correct).

Example: after Passage B — Q: "Kako počinje formalni e-mail?" (Draga Ana, / Poštovana gospođo Horvat, / Halo,)

## Section 3 — Grammar & vocab cloze (20 items, ~12 min)

Fill/MC/reorder mix; distribution pinned to the coverage map: future I ×2 · DAT ×2 · modals ×1 · INS ×2 · GEN pl & numbers ×3 · comparatives/superlatives ×3 · VOC ×1 · imperative/nemoj ×2 · clitics ×2 · aspect ×2.

Example items (this exact style and difficulty):

1. fill — »Sutra ___ ti poslati odgovor.« (ću)
2. fill — »Baka ima pet ___ (mačka).« (mačaka)
3. fill — »U parku nema ___ (pas, pl).« (pasa)
4. MC — »Zagreb je veći ___ Splita.« (od / nego / iz) → od
5. fill — »Zima je ___ (hladan) od jeseni.« (hladnija)
6. fill — »___ (Ivan), dođi na proslavu!« (Ivane)
7. MC — »Nemoj ___ tortu!« (jedi / jesti / jedite) → jesti
8. reorder — dala / mu / ga / je → »Dala mu ga je.«
9. fill — »Vidiš li Anu? — Ne, ne vidim ___.« (je)
10. MC — »Jučer sam ___ knjigu cijeli dan.« (čitao / pročitao) → čitao
11. fill — »Idem u grad ___ (auto).« (autom)
12. fill — »Božić je dvadeset petog ___ (prosinac).« (prosinca)

Plus 8 more of the same kinds covering the remaining pinned slots (DAT: *Šaljem ___ poruku (mama)* → mami; modal: *Moraš ___ (doći/dođi)*; sviđati se; aspect-future; superlative; GEN-pl quantity; clitic placement; vocab MC).

## Section 4 — Speaking (4 prompts, ~8 min, AI-graded)

Learner records; STT transcript + audio features go to the AI grader with the rubric below and the A2 grammar scope. Model answers exist for calibration but any adequate response scores.

- **P1 (self/biography, targets aspect + perfekt):** "Ispričaj svoju priču: gdje si se rodio/rodila, gdje si odrastao/odrasla, što si završio/završila." (4+ sentences)
- **P2 (plans, targets future I + aspect):** "Što ćeš raditi sljedeće godine? Reci tri plana." 
- **P3 (comparison, targets comparatives + GEN pl):** "Usporedi ljeto i zimu — što je bolje i zašto?"
- **P4 (interaction, targets VOC + imperative + clitics):** "Pozovi prijatelja na proslavu: pozdravi ga, reci kada je proslava i što treba donijeti." (leave a voicemail)

**Speaking rubric — each prompt scored 1–5 on each dimension, averaged to the prompt's 5-pt score:**

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task fulfilment | off-task / fragment | covers most of the prompt | fully answers all parts, adequate length |
| Grammatical accuracy (A2 scope) | errors block meaning | frequent errors, meaning survives | minor slips only; target structures attempted and mostly correct |
| Comprehensibility & fluency | hard to follow | understandable with effort/restarts | flows; a sympathetic native follows easily |

Grader instructions: reward *attempted* target structures (a wrong-but-tried clitic cluster beats an avoided one); pronunciation judged only for comprehensibility; ijekavian/diacritics not assessable in speech.

## Section 5 — Guided writing (1 task, 40–60 words, ~5 min, AI-graded)

**Task:** »Napiši e-mail Ani. Pozovi je na svoju proslavu: napiši kada je proslava (datum!), što slavite i što Ana treba donijeti. Počni i završi e-mail kako treba.« (Write Ana an e-mail inviting her to your celebration: date in GEN, what you're celebrating, what to bring; proper opening/closing.)

Forces: salutation (Draga Ana,), date in GEN, imperative or nemoj, a clitic or two (pozivam te / javi mi se), closing (Pozdrav + name). 40–60 words enforced softly (word counter; grader penalizes only <30 or >90).

**Writing rubric — 1–5 each, total /15:**

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task completion | misses most required elements | most elements present (date, invitation, item) | all elements + correct e-mail frame (opening/closing) |
| Grammar | errors obscure meaning | case/verb errors present but text readable | A2 structures used correctly (date GEN, imperative, clitic placement); minor slips only |
| Vocabulary range | repeats a tiny set, English calques | adequate U14/U15 lexicon | varied, natural word choice across units; correct diacritics |

Grader returns: score per dimension + 2–3 line feedback in EN + the corrected text with changes highlighted. The corrected sentences seed cloze SRS cards.

## Scoring, results & follow-up

- Total = listening 10 + reading 10 + cloze 20 + speaking 20 + writing 15 = **75 pts; pass ≥ 53 (70%)**.
- Results screen: per-section bars + per-spine-item breakdown (the coverage map, coloured by performance) → "you're weakest in clitic order" level of specificity.
- Per §8: every wrong item → priority SRS card; weak spine items → AI "Practice more" exercise sets; fail → recommend redoing the weakest units' L5 review lessons + retake after ≥2 days. Pass → unlock B1 Unit 17 with a celebration card (»Čestitamo! Govoriš hrvatski na razini A2.«).
