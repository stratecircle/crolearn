# A2 Unit 13 — Priroda (Nature & Seasons)

## Overview

The fifth A2 grammar block: **GEN plural** completes the genitive story begun in Unit 7 (GEN sg) and Unit 9 (GEN prepositions) — from here the learner can count and quantify ANY noun (pet otoka, puno ljudi). The second half unlocks **comparatives and superlatives**, the engine of opinions: which place is bigger, which season is best. Nature/seasons vocabulary gives maximally concrete things to count and compare; everything feeds the "describe your favourite place" can-do that closes the unit.

## Can-do objectives

- I can name landscapes, animals, and the four seasons
- I can count any noun correctly (jedan pas, dva psa, pet pasa) and use quantity words (puno, malo, nekoliko + GEN pl)
- I can compare two things (Zagreb je veći od Splita / veći nego Split)
- I can say which one is the biggest/best (naj-)
- I can describe a place I love in 4–5 connected sentences

## Lessons

### L1 — Genitiv množine (GEN plural: -a, -i, fleeting a)

- **Grammar micro-point:** GEN pl formation: masc/neut/fem-a → **-a** (grad→gradova, jezero→jezera, planina→planina); fem -i type → **-i** (noć→noći); consonant clusters insert **fleeting a** (pas→pasa, mačka→mačaka, sestra→sestara, momak→momaka, student→studenata); irregulars ljudi (čovjek), djece (djeca). → grammar-reference §3
- **New vocab:** L1 set
- **Required visuals:** GEN-pl column added to the running declension table (endings in GEN green); **GEN-pl fleeting-a table** (NOM sg | GEN sg | GEN pl: pas–psa–pasa · mačka–mačke–mačaka · momak–momka–momaka · sestra–sestre–sestara · student–studenta–studenata); usage-map diagram: "one ending -a, three genders" boxes with arrows to example phrases
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "One small ending and you can count everything in Croatian." → Teach: -a rule across genders + green-highlighted endings → Teach: fleeting-a table (call back to U7: pas→psa — same a, it comes back!) → Examples + TTS: *U parku nema pasa.* (There are no dogs in the park.) · *Selo je blizu planina.* (The village is near the mountains.) · *U jezeru nema riba.* (There are no fish in the lake.) → MC ×3 (pick the GEN pl: mačka → mačke/mačaka/mački) → fill ×3 with nema/blizu/iz + GEN pl → type ×2 (jedna planina → pet ___) → listen-type ×2 (pasa, mačaka — hear the inserted a) → speak: say what your town has none of (*U gradu nema planina.*) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** nema + GEN sg (U7: *nema mlijeka*), blizu/iz + GEN (U9: *blizu hotela*), kilogram sira pattern (U7)
- **SRS:** L1 vocab; cloze cards: "pet ___ (pas)" → pasa; "puno ___ (mačka)" → mačaka; "nema ___ (čovjek, pl)" → ljudi

### L2 — Koliko? (Quantities + numbers with GEN pl)

- **Grammar micro-point:** quantity words (puno, malo, nekoliko, koliko) + numbers 5+ govern **GEN pl**; 2–4 keep GEN sg (paucal); 1 agrees. The full counting system, now explained (U7 taught it as a fixed pattern). → grammar-reference §13
- **New vocab:** L2 set
- **Required visuals:** **numbers→case bracket diagram: 1 → NOM sg (jedan pas) · 2–4 → GEN sg (dva psa) · 5+ → GEN pl (pet pasa)** — the §4-mandated bracket, now with all three branches taught; quantity-words card (puno/malo/nekoliko → GEN pl arrow)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "How many islands does Croatia have? Over a thousand — say it right." → Teach: bracket diagram, worked example jedan pas / dva psa / pet pasa → Examples + TTS: *Na plaži ima puno ljudi.* (There are a lot of people on the beach.) · *Baka Marija ima dva psa i pet mačaka.* (Grandma Marija has two dogs and five cats.) · *Koliko otoka ima Hrvatska?* (How many islands does Croatia have?) → MC ×3 (dva ___ / pet ___ choose form) → Teach: animal vocab grid (icons per animal) → type ×3 production (three horses = tri konja; ten cows = deset krava) → fill ×2: *U šumi ima puno ___ (ptica).* → reorder ×1 → listen-type ×2 (dictation: *pet pasa* · *puno ljudi* — catch the inserted a) → speak: say how many pets you have (*Imam dva psa i pet mačaka.*) → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** numbers 20–1000 (U7), Koliko košta? + prices (U7 — now with eura: *pet eura*), ima/nema (U3/U7)
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; cloze: "dva ___ (pas)" → psa vs "pet ___" → pasa (contrast pair); "puno ___ (čovjek)" → ljudi

### L3 — Toplije i hladnije (Comparatives)

- **Grammar micro-point:** comparative formation: **-iji** (star→stariji, hladan→hladniji, topao→topliji), **-ši/-i with mutation** (lijep→ljepši, visok→viši, dubok→dublji, kratak→kraći, brz→brži); irregular four: dobar→**bolji**, loš→**gori**, velik→**veći**, malen/mali→**manji**. Comparatives decline like adjectives. → grammar-reference §4
- **New vocab:** L3 set (seasons + adjectives that will be compared)
- **Required visuals:** before→after transformation cards (star → star**iji** with moving-suffix highlight, per §4 word-order/transformation row); irregular-four warning card (dobar/loš/velik/malen with ❌ *dobriji* crossed out); seasons wheel diagram (proljeće 🌸 → ljeto ☀️ → jesen 🍂 → zima ❄️) with a temperature arc
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Winter is cold. Autumn is colder. Now you can rank the world." → Teach: -iji rule + transformation cards → Examples + TTS: *Danas je hladnije.* (It's colder today.) · *Velebit je visok, a Dinara je još viša.* (Velebit is high, and Dinara is even higher.) · *Kava u Zagrebu je dobra, ali kava kod bake je bolja.* (Coffee in Zagreb is good, but coffee at grandma's is better.) → MC ×3 (pick the comparative) → Teach: irregular four + seasons wheel, ljeti/zimi chunks → match: adjective ↔ comparative ×1 → type ×3 (cold → colder: hladan → hladnije/hladniji by gender) → fill ×2 → listen-type ×2 (*Danas je hladnije.* · *Kava kod bake je bolja.*) → speak: compare today and yesterday aloud (*Danas je toplije.*) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** weather + past narration (U8: *Jučer je padala kiša, danas je hladnije.*), sviđa mi se (U10: *Sviđa mi se jesen.*)
- **SRS:** L3 vocab; cloze the irregular four (dobar → ___); comparative-formation cards (visok → viši, topao → topliji)

### L4 — Najviša planina (Superlatives + od/nego comparisons)

- **Grammar micro-point:** superlative = **naj-** + comparative (najbolji, najviši, najljepši); comparison standards: **od + GEN** (veći od Splita) or **nego + NOM** (veći nego Split). → grammar-reference §4
- **New vocab:** L4 set
- **Required visuals:** three-step podium diagram (visok → viši → najviši on 3rd/2nd/1st place); od/nego twin-track card: *od + Splita* (GEN, green) vs *nego + Split* (NOM, blue) — same meaning, two roads
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "What's the highest mountain in Croatia? After this lesson you can argue about it." → Teach: naj- + podium → Examples + TTS: *Dinara je najviša planina u Hrvatskoj.* (Dinara is the highest mountain in Croatia.) · *More je toplije od rijeke.* (The sea is warmer than the river.) · *Zagreb je veći nego Split.* (Zagreb is bigger than Split.) → MC ×3 (od + which form?) → type ×2 (deeper than the lake → dublje od jezera) → Teach: comparison-adjective vocab with icon pairs (visok/nizak ⛰️, brz/spor 🐇🐢) → fill ×3 (*Rijeka je ___ (dug) od jezera* — dulja/duža both accepted; *corrected 2026-07-23: spec's original standard »od potoka« used untaught potok*) → reorder ×1: je / od / more / rijeke / toplije → listen-type ×2 (*More je toplije od rijeke.* · *Dinara je najviša planina.*) → speak: compare two cities you know (*Zagreb je veći od Splita.*) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** GEN sg endings (U7 — od + Splita/rijeke/jezera), INS transport (U12: *Vlakom je brže nego autom.* — as a heard chunk), future I (U9: *Sutra će biti toplije.*)
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; cloze: "naj + bolji = ___"; "veći ___ Splita (od/nego?)" → od; production cards for podium triples

### L5 — Najljepše mjesto (Describing places, review)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new machinery — integration: GEN pl + quantities + comparatives/superlatives in connected description. Pattern sentence: *[Mjesto] je najljepše jer ima puno [GEN pl].*
- **New vocab:** L5 set
- **Required visuals:** semantic landscape grid (one illustrated scene: nebo, drvo, trava, cvijet, kamen labelled in place, gender-coloured); unit mega-recap table (GEN pl endings + bracket diagram + comparative ladder on one slide)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS plays a 4-sentence description of Plitvice — "by the end of today, you write one of these about YOUR place." → Teach: landscape grid vocab → Examples + TTS: *Pogled na more je predivan.* (The view of the sea is wonderful.) · *Zrak na planini je svjež.* (The air on the mountain is fresh.) · *Najljepše godišnje doba je proljeće.* (The most beautiful season is spring.) → MC ×2 → fill: guided description with blanks (quantity + GEN pl, one comparative, one superlative) → type ×2 → listen-type ×2 → speak: describe your favourite place in 3 sentences (self-assess vs model) → integration ×3 with story cast (*Ana voli more više nego planine.*) → unit-review slide set (8 quiz slides sampling L1–L4) → Recap
- **Interleaving:** u/na + LOC (U5: *na planini, u šumi*), perfekt (U8: *Prošlog ljeta smo bili na moru.*), voljeti + ACC (U4)
- **SRS:** L5 vocab; cloze description-frame cards; comparative/superlative mixed cloze

## Story — «Izlet na Plitvička jezera» (A trip to the Plitvice Lakes)

Baka Marija tells Tom that Plitvice is the most beautiful place in Croatia, so Tom, Ana and Marko take a weekend trip. They compare everything — the lakes are deeper, the air is fresher, the water is colder than the sea. Tom counts waterfalls and asks whether there are bears in the forest; Ana laughs and says there are more tourists than animals. 150–300 words, present + perfekt + a little future I; GEN pl and comparatives on every screen.
Cast: Tom, Ana, Marko (baka Marija in the opening scene). New glossed words (≤5): slap (waterfall), turist (tourist), mir (peace). *(Corrected 2026-07-23: izlet was taught in U8 L3; medvjed is now L2 vocab — see vocab correction note.)*
Sample sentences: »Na Plitvicama ima šesnaest jezera i puno slapova.« · »Ima li ovdje medvjeda?« pita Tom. »Ovdje ima više turista nego životinja!« govori Ana.
Comprehension: 5 MC questions (Kamo idu? Što je dublje — jezero ili more? Koga se Tom boji? …).

## Unit test blueprint (16–18 items, pass ≥ 70%)

1. **Vocab (5):** match HR↔EN ×2 (animals, landscape), MC ×3 (seasons, adjectives)
2. **Grammar (6):** GEN pl fill ×2 (pet ___ (mačka); puno ___ (čovjek)), bracket MC ×1 (dva psa/pasa/pas), comparative fill ×2 (incl. one irregular: dobar → ___), od/nego MC ×1
3. **Listening (4):** listen-type ×2 (dictation: *pet pasa*, *najljepše jezero*), listen + MC meaning ×2
4. **Reading (3):** 4-line description of a place + 3 MC comprehension
Example item: fill — »Zima je ___ (hladan) od jeseni.« (hladnija) · listen-type — TTS says *puno mačaka*, learner types it.
