# A2 Unit 12 — Posao (Work & Study)

## Overview

The INSTRUMENTAL unit — the sixth case, and mercifully the friendliest: one dominant ending per gender and two crystal-clear jobs (with-someone, by-means-of). The long-promised **idem vlakom / autom** (banned since U5!) finally unlocks. Work and university vocabulary plus a job-interview script make this the most "adult life in Croatia" unit of A2, and set up U16's biography capstone. Uses everything through U11 (futur I, DAT, modals).

## Can-do objectives

- I can say who I do things with (s prijateljem, s kolegicom)
- I can say how I travel and what I work/write with (vlakom, računalom)
- I can describe my job and studies (Radim kao…, Studiram…)
- I can handle basic job-interview questions in Croatian
- I can talk about workdays combining times, obligations, and plans (moram, ću)

## Lessons

### L1 — S kolegom u uredu (INS singular: company)

- **Grammar micro-point:** INS sg endings: masc/neut **-om** (soft stems **-em**: prijateljem, mužem), fem **-om**; kolega (masc -a noun) → kolegom. Use #1: **s/sa + INS** = with (company/accompaniment). **sa** before s, š, z, ž (sa šefom, sa sestrom; fixed: sa mnom). → grammar-reference §3, §14
- **Required visuals:** INS-teal declension table appended to the growing case table (now 6 columns — only VOC greyed); **INS usage-map diagram** (two teal boxes: WITH s/sa + company · BY bare-INS means [preview — full contrast diagram in L2], each arrowed to an example phrase); soft-stem -em spotlight card (č ć đ š ž j lj nj + c → -em); s/sa switch card
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Radim s Anom. Kava s mlijekom. One ending, huge unlock." → Teach: INS table + s/sa rule → Examples + TTS: Radim s kolegom. Ana razgovara sa šeficom. Tom pije kavu s mlijekom. (U4 flashback: kava s mlijekom was a chunk — now decoded!) → MC ×3 (pick form: s ___ — prijatelj/prijateljem) → fill ×3 → type ×2: "with a colleague", "coffee with sugar" (sa šećerom — sa before š!) → reorder: s / radim / kolegicom / u uredu → listen-type ×2 (*Radim s kolegom.* · *Pijem kavu s mlijekom.*) → interleave ×3 (U10 DAT contrast: dajem kolegi vs radim s kolegom) → speak: Radim s prijateljem. → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U10 DAT (same nouns, different case — contrast drill), U4 chunk payoff
- **SRS:** vocab + INS cloze cards; s/sa judgment cards
- **Watch-out (NotesDoc):** ~~s kolega~~ ❌; ~~sa Anom~~ ❌ (sa only before s/š/z/ž — s Anom ✅); DAT/INS -om confusion doesn't exist — but DAT fem -i vs INS fem -om does (kolegici vs s kolegicom)

### L2 — Idem vlakom (INS of means — NO s!)

- **Grammar micro-point:** Use #2: bare INS (NO preposition) for means/instrument — transport (putujem vlakom, idem autom) and tools (pišem olovkom, radim računalom). **The U5 ban lifts: this is why we waited.** Exception: pješice (adverb, no case). → grammar-reference §14
- **Required visuals:** the mandated **contrast diagram**: two panels — 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 "S + INS = WITH someone" (s Anom) vs 🚆 "bare INS = BY means" (vlakom, ~~s vlakom~~ ❌ crossed out big); transport icon row with INS forms
- **Slide arc:** Hook: U5 flashback card ("remember: čekam tramvaj, never idem tramvajem — until today") → Teach: bare-INS rule + contrast diagram → Examples + TTS: Putujem vlakom u Split. Idem na posao biciklom. Pišem zadaću računalom. Ana ide pješice. → MC ×4 (s or no s? idem ___ autom) → fill ×3 → type ×2: "I travel by plane", "I'm going with Marko by car" (s Markom autom — both uses in one sentence!) → listen-type ×2 → interleave ×3 (U9 travel: Putovat ću vlakom — futur + INS) → speak: Idem na posao tramvajem. → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U9 futur I + travel, U5 transport vocab
- **SRS:** transport INS forms; s-vs-bare judgment cards (high priority — THE unit trap)
- **Watch-out:** ~~idem s autobusom~~ ❌ (English "by/with" interference) → autobusom ✅; auto is masculine (novim autom)

### L3 — Radim kao konobar (Professions & raditi kao)

- **Grammar micro-point:** **raditi kao + NOM** (Radim kao konobar — kao takes NOM, a relief!); profession pairs m/f (recycled from U2, now systematized: -ica, -ka feminines); postati + NOM preview chunk (Želim postati liječnik). → grammar-reference §2
- **Required visuals:** profession pair grid (m/f columns, suffix patterns highlighted); "kao = NOM" green-flag card (contrast card: s kolegom INS, kao konobar NOM)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: story-cast job parade via TTS (Marko radi kao konobar. Ana će raditi kao učiteljica.) → Teach: raditi kao + NOM + pair grid → MC ×3 → Teach: workday frames with modals + futur (Moram raditi u subotu. Počinjem u osam. Zarađujem dobro.) → fill ×3 → type ×2: "She works as a doctor", "I want to become a programmer" → match: profession ↔ workplace (kuhar↔restoran, učiteljica↔škola) → listen-type ×2 (*Ana radi kao učiteljica.* · *Marko zarađuje dobro.*) → interleave ×3 (U11 modals: Moram ići liječniku — DAT!) → speak: Radim kao [your job]. / Studiram. → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U11 modals, U10 DAT persons, U2 professions
- **SRS:** profession pairs; kao-frame cloze
- **Watch-out:** ~~Radim kao konobarom~~ ❌ (INS overreach — kao takes NOM); plaća vs plaćati mixup

### L4 — Na fakultetu (University & studying)

- **Grammar micro-point:** studirati + ACC (Studiram medicinu) vs učiti (study/learn generally); university LOC/ACC territory recycled (na fakultetu, na predavanju, idem na ispit); položiti ispit chunk (aspect silently correct — analysis waits for U16). → grammar-reference §14
- **Required visuals:** student-week grid (predavanja timetable — times from U6, days recycled); studirati/učiti contrast card (major vs activity); fakultet na-word flashback (U5 list)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: Ana's exam week via TTS → Teach: studirati vs učiti + examples: Ana studira medicinu. Učim hrvatski svaki dan. Sutra imam ispit. → MC ×3 → Teach: exam frames (Moram položiti ispit. Učit ću u knjižnici s kolegicom.) → fill ×3 (timetable prompts) → type ×2: "I study economics" (Studiram ekonomiju), "We're studying in the library" → listen-type ×2 → interleave ×3 (U6 times: Predavanje počinje u devet; U11 treba mi: Treba mi pauza) → reorder: ću / s / učiti / kolegom / navečer → speak: say what and where you study (*Studiram ekonomiju.* / *Učim hrvatski svaki dan.*) → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U6 timetable machinery, U11 DAT experiencer, U5 na-words
- **SRS:** uni vocab; studirati+ACC cloze
- **Watch-out:** ~~studiram na medicini~~ ❌ → studiram medicinu ✅; ~~učim medicinu na fakultetu~~ (fine but flag nuance); ispit se polaže — passive se preview NOT taught, avoided in content

### L5 — Razgovor za posao (The job interview — integration)

- **Grammar micro-point:** none new — the interview script assembling the whole A2-so-far toolkit: introduce yourself (U1/U2), experience in past (U8 perfekt), obligations (U11 modals), plans (U9 futur), with-whom/by-what (U12 INS). Formal Vi throughout (register recycled from U1, full treatment B2 U30).
- **Required visuals:** interview flow map (greet → self-intro → experience → why-you → questions → closing chunk Javit ću se); "Vi-mode ON" banner card (all script lines in Vi)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS full interview dialogue, Tom interviewing at a Zagreb tvrtka → Teach: script beats + key frames (Imam iskustvo. Radio sam kao konobar dvije godine. Mogu početi u ponedjeljak. Putovat ću tramvajem.) → MC ×2 (pragmatics: right response to interviewer lines) → fill: dialogue blanks → reorder ×2 → type ×2: "I worked as a waiter", "I can start on Monday" → listen-type ×2 → integration: build YOUR mini-interview (guided chain, 6 slides) → speak ×2: self-intro + one experience line (Vi-register) → unit-review set: 8 quiz slides sampling L1–L4 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** whole unit + U8 perfekt + U9 futur + U11 modals
- **SRS:** interview chunks; learner's own lines as custom cards
- **Watch-out:** ti-forms slipping into a Vi dialogue (drill the switch); ~~Ja sam radio kao konobarom~~ ❌ double error card

## Story — «Tomov prvi posao» (Tom's first job)

Tom applies to teach English at a Zagreb language school. Ana helps polish his životopis (Piše ga računalom!), he travels tramvajem, interviews with the šefica (formal Vi), narrates his experience in perfekt, and gets the call — Javit ću se… i javila se! He celebrates with Marko — kava sa šlagom. 200–250 words, INS woven throughout both uses.
Cast: Tom, Ana, Marko + nova šefica (unnamed recurring extra). Glossed (≤5): škola stranih jezika (language school, chunk), uspjeh (success), čestitati (to congratulate — formally taught later in U14), nervozan (nervous), šlag (whipped cream).
Sample sentences: »Tom putuje tramvajem na razgovor za posao.« · »Radio sam kao učitelj engleskog u Kanadi« — kaže Tom šefici.
Comprehension: 4 MC (Čime Tom putuje? S kim razgovara? Što je radio u Kanadi? …)
