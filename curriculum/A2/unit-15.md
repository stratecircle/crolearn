# A2 Unit 15 — Komunikacija (Phone, Mail, Internet)

## Overview

The ninth A2 grammar block: **object clitic pronouns** — the little words (ga, mi, je…) that make Croatian sound like Croatian. Building on the learner's long acquaintance with second-position clitics (sam/si/je since U1/U8, ću since U9), this unit makes the object forms systematic: ACC clitics, then DAT clitics, then the basic cluster with its fixed internal order. Phone/mail/internet vocabulary is the perfect habitat — real communication is FULL of "call me", "send it to her", "I'll text you". Full cluster mastery (li, GEN, se edge cases) is a B1 U23 target; here we lay the rails.

## Can-do objectives

- I can replace objects with pronouns (Vidim Anu → Vidim je; Ne vidim ga)
- I can say to whom (Javi mi se! Šaljem ti poruku.)
- I can order clitics correctly in a cluster (Dala mu ga je.)
- I can make and answer a phone call with the standard phrases
- I can write a short e-mail or text message with a proper opening and closing

## New vocabulary (~40 items)

**L1 (phone):** mobitel (m, mobile phone) · poziv (m, call) · poruka (f, message/text) · broj (m, number) · nazvati (to call up) · čuti (to hear) · poslati (to send) · primiti (to receive)

**L2 (responding):** javiti se (to get in touch, + DAT) · odgovoriti (to answer, + DAT) · odgovor (m, answer) · pitanje (n, question) · vijest (f, news item) · slika (f, picture) · razgovor (m, conversation) · razgovarati (to converse)

**L3 (internet):** internet (m) · mreža (f, network) · e-mail (m) · adresa (f, address) · lozinka (f, password) · stranica (f, page/website) · aplikacija (f, app) · objaviti (to post/publish)

**L4 (messages & phone phrases):** halo (hello? — phone only) · poštovani/poštovana (respected — formal Dear) · pozdrav (m, greeting/regards) · potpis (m, signature) · pismo (n, letter) · hitno (urgent(ly)) · zauzet (busy/engaged) · ostaviti (to leave [a message])

**L5 (staying in touch):** veza (f, connection) · objava (f, post) · pratiti (to follow) · dopisivati se (to text back and forth, correspond) · video-poziv (m, video call) · dijeliti (dijelim, to share)

## Lessons

### L1 — Vidim ga! (ACC clitic pronouns)

- **Grammar micro-point:** ACC clitics **me, te, ga, je, nas, vas, ih** replace known objects; they are unstressed and can NEVER stand first (same law as sam/je from U1/U8). Full forms (mene, tebe, njega…) exist for stress/after prepositions — shown, not drilled. → grammar-reference §5, §12
- **New vocab:** L1 set
- **Required visuals:** pronoun grid full↔clitic (mene→me, njega→ga, nju→je, njih→ih; clitics in ACC red); **train-carriage diagram (first appearance)**: engine = first word, one wagon "ACC" for now — the train grows over L2–L3; ⚠️ "never first" card (callback to *Sam Ana* ❌ from U1)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "»Where's my phone? I can't see it.« — one tiny word does 'it'." → Teach: clitic grid + never-first law → Examples + TTS: *Gdje je mobitel? Ne vidim ga.* (Where's the phone? I can't see it.) · *Čuješ li me?* (Can you hear me?) · *Marko me zove svaki dan.* (Marko calls me every day.) → MC ×3 (which clitic replaces "Anu"? → je) → Teach: phone vocab with icons → fill ×3 (*Imaš li broj? — Da, imam ___.* → ga) → type ×2 (replace the object) → reorder ×1: me / li / čuješ → listen-type ×2 → speak: answer *Čuješ li me?* aloud (*Čujem te!*) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** zvati se (U1) vs zvati koga (now), second-position je (U8: *Zvao se Marko*), masc animate ACC = GEN (U4: Vidim Marka → Vidim ga)
- **SRS:** L1 vocab; cloze: "Vidim Anu → Vidim ___" (je); "Ne čujem ___ (tebe)" (te)

### L2 — Javi mi se! (DAT clitic pronouns)

- **Grammar micro-point:** DAT clitics **mi, ti, mu, joj, nam, vam, im** — the "to me / to you" words; verbs that love them: javiti se, odgovoriti, poslati, reći. Order preview: DAT rides before se (Javi **mi se**!). → grammar-reference §5, §12
- **New vocab:** L2 set
- **Required visuals:** DAT clitic grid (meni→mi, njemu→mu, njoj→joj — clitics in DAT orange); train gains its DAT wagon **in front of** ACC and se; verb-valency cards (javiti se + komu, odgovoriti + komu with orange slots)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "»Text me!« »I'll answer you tomorrow.« — Croatian does this in one syllable." → Teach: DAT grid → Examples + TTS: *Javi mi se!* (Get in touch with me! / Text me!) · *Odgovorit ću ti sutra.* (I'll answer you tomorrow.) · *Pošalji joj sliku!* (Send her the picture!) → MC ×3 (to him = mu/ga/mi?) → Teach: javiti se / odgovoriti valency cards → fill ×3 (*Baka ___ šalje čestitku.* (nama) → nam) → type ×2 → reorder ×1: mi / se / javi → listen-type ×2 → speak: tell a friend to send you the picture (*Pošalji mi sliku!*) → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** DAT nouns (U10: *dajem mami* → *dajem joj*), čestitam ti chunk now explained (U14), imperative hosting clitics (U14: *Pošalji joj sliku!*)
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; cloze: "šaljem poruku Ani → šaljem ___ poruku" (joj); "Javi ___ ___!" (mi se — order!)

### L3 — Dala mu ga je (Clitic placement & the basic cluster)

- **Grammar micro-point:** clitics live in **second position**, in fixed internal order: **li → aux → DAT → ACC/GEN → se → je** — and **je goes LAST** (Dala mu ga **je**); se + je → je drops (*Javila se*, never *se je*). Drilled subset: aux + DAT + ACC. → grammar-reference §12
- **New vocab:** none new — this lesson is pure order (light review vocab from L1–L2 recycled in every item)
- **Required visuals:** **the full train-carriage diagram**: fixed coupled wagons li → aux → DAT → ACC/GEN → se → je behind the "first stressed word" engine (per §4 clitic-order row) — THE visual of the unit, reproduced in notes; je-caboose card (je always the last wagon); se+je collision card (*se je* → je falls off)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS plays *Dala mu ga je.* — "four words, three of them clitics, one rigid order. All aboard." → Teach: train diagram, wagon by wagon → Examples + TTS: *Dao sam mu ga.* (I gave it to him.) · *Dala mu ga je.* (She gave it to him — je last!) · *Ana se javila baki.* (Ana got in touch with grandma — se je → je drops.) → MC ×3 (pick the correct order) → reorder ×3 (sam / ga / mu / dao; je / mu / ga / dala) → Teach: second-position with longer openings (*Sutra ću ti poslati odgovor.*) → fill ×2 → type ×1 (translate "I sent it to her yesterday" → Jučer sam joj ga poslao.) → listen-type ×2 → speak: say *Dala mu ga je.* aloud to the train rhythm (the li-aux-DAT-ACC-se-je chant) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** perfekt je second-position (U8 — the same law, now with wagons), future ću (U9: *Sutra ću ti…*), poslati/primiti (this unit L1)
- **SRS:** cloze order cards: "Dala ___ ___ ___ (je, mu, ga)" → mu ga je; "Javila ___ (se je / se?)" → se; reorder-style production cards

### L4 — Halo? Poštovani… (Phone calls & writing messages)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new machinery — the phone-call script and the e-mail/message template as fixed pragmatic frames (formal vs informal register riding on U1's ti/Vi); capital **Vam/Vas** in formal writing. Clitics from L1–L3 everywhere.
- **New vocab:** L4 set
- **Required visuals:** phone-call flow diagram (zvoni → *Halo?* → *Tko je?* → identify → matter → *Čujemo se!*); e-mail anatomy diagram (labelled skeleton: Poštovana gospođo / Draga Ana → body → Srdačan pozdrav / Pozdrav → potpis) — formal and informal columns side by side
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS plays a ringing phone and a full mini-call — "both ends of this call are yours by tonight." → Teach: call flow → Examples + TTS: *Halo, tko je?* (Hello, who is it?) · *Linija je zauzeta.* (The line is busy.) · *Poštovana gospođo Horvat, šaljem Vam recept za tortu. Srdačan pozdrav, Ana.* (Dear Mrs Horvat, I'm sending you the recipe for the cake. Kind regards, Ana.) → MC ×2 (formal or informal opening?) → Teach: e-mail anatomy → fill ×3 (complete a short e-mail skeleton) → reorder ×1 → type ×2 (write a 2-line text to Ana: greeting + javi mi se) → listen-type ×2 (a phone number — digits!) → speak: answer a ringing phone in role (*Halo, tko je?* → identify yourself) → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** VOC in salutations (U14: *Poštovani gospodine!* / *Draga bako,*), courtesy chunks (U1: molim, hvala, oprostite), numbers read aloud (U7 — phone numbers)
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; frame cards (formal opening ↔ closing pairs); "javit ću ___ se" (Vam) formal card

### L5 — Ostanimo u vezi (Review & integrated practice)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new grammar — integration under fluency pressure: every task forces a clitic choice AND a placement choice at once (the two hard things together).
- **New vocab:** L5 set (small by design — review lesson)
- **Required visuals:** train diagram reprise as an interactive drag-the-wagon summary; unit mega-recap card (ACC grid + DAT grid + train on one slide)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Baka Marija has a mobitel now. Someone has to answer her." → Teach: L5 vocab (pratiti, objava, dopisivati se, veza) → Examples + TTS: *Nazvat ću je večeras.* (I'll call her tonight.) · *Jesi li mi poslao poruku?* (Did you send me the message?) · *Dopisujemo se svaki dan.* (We text each other every day.) → speed round: MC ×3 + reorder ×2 + fill ×3 mixing L1–L4 patterns → listen-type ×2 → speak: answer a TTS phone call out loud (self-assess vs model script) → integration ×3 with cast (*Ana objavljuje sliku; Tom je prati.*) → unit-review slide set (8 quiz slides sampling L1–L4) → Recap
- **Interleaving:** future I + clitics (U9+L3: *Nazvat ću je*), imperative + clitics (U14+L2: *Javi mi se!*), sviđati se (U10: *Sviđa mi se objava.*)
- **SRS:** L5 vocab; mixed cluster clozes; personalized card "moj broj je ___"

## Story — «Baka Marija i mobitel» (Grandma Marija and the mobile phone)

For her birthday (Unit 14 callback) baka Marija got a mobitel from Tom. Now he teaches her to use it: how to answer, how to send a picture, how to video-call. Baka is suspicious, then delighted — soon she calls everyone daily and sends Ana pictures of her cakes. The story is saturated with clitics in honest, natural positions. 150–300 words.
Cast: baka Marija, Tom, Ana (Marko on the receiving end of one call). New glossed words (≤5): punjač (charger), ekran (screen), uključiti (to switch on), zvoniti/zvoni (to ring).
Sample sentences: »Kako ga uključujem?« pita baka. »Evo, ovako — nemoj ga gasiti.« · Baka zove Anu: »Ano, čuješ li me? Šaljem ti sliku torte!«
Comprehension: 5 MC questions (Što je baka dobila? Tko je uči? Što šalje Ani? …).

## Unit test blueprint (17–19 items, pass ≥ 70%)

1. **Vocab (5):** match HR↔EN ×2 (phone, internet words), MC ×3
2. **Grammar (7):** ACC clitic fill ×2 (Vidim Anu → Vidim ___), DAT clitic fill ×2, cluster reorder ×2 (dala / mu / ga / je; li / jesi / mi / poslao / poruku), se+je MC ×1 (Javila se / Javila se je?)
3. **Listening (4):** listen-type ×2 (*Javi mi se!*, a phone number), listen + MC meaning ×2
4. **Reading (3):** a short e-mail (formal) + 3 MC comprehension (tko piše, komu, zašto)
Example item: reorder — build »Dala mu ga je.« from shuffled words · fill — »Poštovana gospođo, javljam ___ se zbog stana.« (Vam)

## NotesDoc focus per lesson

- **L1:** full pronoun table full-form ↔ clitic, all persons (incl. the je/ju note: ju avoids clash next to aux je — receptive awareness only); mistakes: clitic first (*Ga vidim* ❌ → Vidim ga ✅), using full form unstressed (*Vidim njega* when nothing is contrasted — sounds pushy), English "it" → assuming one form (ga m/n vs je f)
- **L2:** DAT grid complete; mistakes: *Javi se mi* ❌ → Javi mi se ✅ (DAT before se), ACC where DAT belongs (*odgovorit ću te* ❌ → ti ✅), mu vs ga confusion (to him vs him)
- **L3:** THE train diagram reproduced full-width + the two laws in a box (second position; je last); mistakes: *Dala je mu ga* ❌ → Dala mu ga je ✅, *Dao sam ga mu* ❌ → sam mu ga ✅ (DAT before ACC), *Javila se je* ❌ → Javila se ✅; hook: chant "li-aux-DAT-ACC-se-JE" to the rhythm of a train
- **L4:** both e-mail skeletons (formal/informal) printable; mistakes: *Poštovani Ana* ❌ (mixing registers) → Draga Ana / Poštovana gospođo ✅, lowercase vam in formal writing (capital Vam!), *Halo* in e-mails (phone-only word)
- **L5:** one-page unit summary: both grids + train + call script + e-mail skeleton; common mistake roundup drawn from the learner's own retry-queue stats
