# A2 Unit 16 — Moja priča (Biography & Plans)

## Overview

The A2 finale and the door to B1: **verb aspect**, introduced gently — pairs learned as vocabulary (pisati/napisati), then felt in the past (was writing vs wrote-and-finished), then in the future (radit ću vs napravit ću). No pair-formation theory yet (that's B1 U17); here the learner just starts *choosing*. The biography theme makes aspect meaningful: a life story is exactly the dance of long processes (odrastao sam) and single events (rodio sam se, preselio sam se). The unit ends with the learner telling their own story — past, present, and plans — using everything from Units 1–16.

## Can-do objectives

- I can recognize imperfective/perfective pairs and say what each half means
- I can choose the right aspect in the past (Pisao sam pismo. / Napisao sam pismo.)
- I can choose the right aspect in the future (Radit ću cijelo ljeto. / Napravit ću plan.)
- I can narrate the main events of a life (birth, growing up, school, moves, marriage)
- I can tell my own story and my plans in 6–8 connected sentences

## Lessons

### L1 — Dva glagola za jednu radnju (Aspect pairs as vocabulary)

- **Grammar micro-point:** almost every Croatian verb comes in a pair: **imperfective** = process/habit (wavy line), **perfective** = completed whole (point) — pisati/napisati, čitati/pročitati, kupovati/kupiti, davati/dati, gledati/pogledati, počinjati/početi. Perfective present ≠ "now": after želim/moram/mogu, perfectives are at home (*moram kupiti*). → grammar-reference §11
- **New vocab:** L1 set (the perfective partners of long-known imperfectives)
- **Required visuals:** **aspect timeline diagram** (per §4): wavy ongoing line 〰️ over "pisati" vs point/checkmark •✓ over "napisati" — THE visual of the unit, reused in L2–L3; pair-cards deck visual (each card two-sided: impf side wavy, pf side checked); "perfective present is not now" warning card
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Croatian has two verbs where English has one — and the choice tells your listener whether you FINISHED." → Teach: timeline diagram with pisati/napisati → Examples + TTS: *Svaki dan čitam knjige. Danas želim pročitati cijelu knjigu.* (I read every day. Today I want to read the whole book — to the end.) · *Kupujem kruh svaki dan. Danas moram kupiti i tortu.* (I buy bread every day. Today I also have to buy a cake.) · *Navečer gledam televiziju. Pogledaj ovo!* (In the evenings I watch TV. Look at this!) → MC ×3 (wavy or point? pick the aspect for the context) → Teach: the pair deck (all 8 pairs of the day) → match: impf ↔ pf pairs → fill ×3 (modal + which infinitive?) → type ×2 → listen-type ×2 (*Danas moram kupiti tortu.* · *Pogledaj ovo!*) → speak: one habit + one today-goal aloud (*Svaki dan čitam. Danas želim pročitati cijelu knjigu.*) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** modals + infinitive (U11: *moram kupiti*), imperative (U14: *Pogledaj ovo!*), htjeti/željeti + inf (U4)
- **SRS:** pair cards both directions (pisati → pf? napisati → impf?); cloze: "Svaki dan ___ (čitati/pročitati?) novine" → čitam-type context cards

### L2 — Pisao sam / napisao sam (Aspect in the past)

- **Grammar micro-point:** in the perfekt, aspect carries the story: imperfective past = ongoing/background/duration (*Pisao sam pismo cijelo jutro* — was writing), perfective past = done, result exists (*Napisao sam pismo* — it's written). Duration phrases (cijeli dan, dva sata) pull imperfective; "and then / finally / already" pull perfective. → grammar-reference §11, §8
- **New vocab:** L2 set
- **Required visuals:** timeline diagram in past tense mode (wavy line stretching across "cijelo jutro" vs a point with a ✓ and a finished letter icon); context-magnet card (cijeli dan / dva sata → 〰️; napokon / i onda → •✓); life-events icon strip (roditi se 👶 → odrasti 🌱 → upoznati 🤝 → preseliti se 📦)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "»Pisao sam pismo« and »Napisao sam pismo« are different mornings. Hear the difference." → Teach: past timeline + context magnets → Examples + TTS: *Pisao sam pismo cijelo jutro.* (I was writing the letter all morning.) · *Napisao sam pismo i poslao ga baki.* (I wrote the letter and sent it to grandma.) · *Ana je pročitala knjigu za dva dana.* (Ana read the whole book in two days.) → MC ×3 (choose aspect from context) → Teach: life-event verbs (all naturally perfective — a life event happens ONCE) → fill ×3 (*Baka je ___ (odrasti) u Slavoniji.* → odrasla) → type ×2 → reorder ×1 → listen-type ×2 → speak: narrate yesterday with one impf + one pf (*Pisao sam pismo cijelo jutro — i napisao sam ga!*) → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** perfekt formation + agreement (U8: pisao/pisala/pisali), clitic cluster (U15: *poslao ga baki*, *Ana je pročitala…*), za + ACC time (U9 travel chunks)
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; context clozes ("cijeli dan sam ___ (pisati/napisati)" → pisao); "rodio ___ se u…" personalized card

### L3 — Radit ću / napravit ću (Aspect in the future)

- **Grammar micro-point:** future I works with both aspects: imperfective future = will be doing / regularly (*Radit ću cijelo ljeto.*), perfective future = will get it done (*Napravit ću plan.*). Same context magnets as L2, now pointing forward. Spelling review: pisat ću, pročitat ću (-ti drops i), doći ću (-ći keeps). → grammar-reference §11, §9
- **New vocab:** L3 set
- **Required visuals:** timeline diagram in future mode (wavy line into the horizon vs a flag planted at a point ahead); plans board visual (cilj 🎯, san 💭, plan 📋 as sticky notes each with a future sentence)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Your plans deserve the right aspect: will you be working, or will you get it DONE?" → Teach: future timeline → Examples + TTS: *Radit ću cijelo ljeto.* (I'll be working all summer.) · *Napravit ću plan do petka.* (I'll have a plan made by Friday.) · *Knjigu ću pročitati do subote.* (I'll finish the book by Saturday.) → MC ×3 (choose aspect for the plan) → Teach: plans vocab + board → fill ×3 (*___ ću (učiti/naučiti) hrvatski svaki dan.* → Učit) → type ×2 (translate "I'll write to you tomorrow" → Pisat ću ti sutra. / Napisat ću ti sutra. — both accepted, feedback explains the nuance) → reorder ×1: ću / do / plan / petka / napraviti → listen-type ×2 (*Radit ću cijelo ljeto.* · *Napravit ću plan do petka.*) → speak ×1 (say one real plan) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** future I forms + spelling (U9: pisat ću), do + GEN deadlines (U9: do petka), clitics in future (U15: *Pisat ću ti*)
- **SRS:** L3 vocab; future-aspect context clozes; "moj cilj je ___" personalized card

### L4 — Životna priča (Narrating a life)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new machinery — the biography schema: a chain of perfective milestones (rodio sam se → odrastao sam → završio sam školu → preselio sam se) on an imperfective background (živio sam, radio sam). Sequencing words: prvo, onda, poslije, na kraju.
- **New vocab:** L4 set
- **Required visuals:** life-timeline diagram (a single horizontal life line: perfective events as pinned points 👶🎓💍📦, imperfective stretches as shaded wavy bands beneath — the unit's timeline visual at full power); biography sentence-frame ladder (Rodio/Rodila sam se u… → Odrastao/Odrasla sam u… → Završio/Završila sam…)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS plays a 5-sentence life story — "this is the shape of every biography in Croatian." → Teach: life-timeline diagram → Examples + TTS: *Rodio sam se u Kanadi.* (I was born in Canada.) · *Baka Marija je odrasla u Slavoniji.* (Grandma Marija grew up in Slavonia.) · *Ana je završila školu i preselila se u Zagreb.* (Ana finished school and moved to Zagreb.) → MC ×2 → fill ×3 (complete a cast member's biography) → Teach: CV vocab (životopis, iskustvo) → type ×2 (two sentences of your own life) → reorder ×1 → listen-type ×2 → speak: say the first two sentences of your life story (*Rodio/Rodila sam se u… Odrastao/Odrasla sam u…*) → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** iz + GEN origin (U2/U9: *iz Kanade*), u + LOC places (U5: *u Slavoniji, u Zagrebu*), se + je drops (U15: *preselila se* — no je after se… note the shared aux)
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; biography-frame clozes (*Rodio sam se u ___*); "završiti/završavati" pair card

### L5 — Moja priča (My story & plans — unit and LEVEL review)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new grammar — the capstone: one connected narrative from birth to plans, past (aspect-correct) → present → future (aspect-correct). This is the A2 exit performance and the direct rehearsal for the checkpoint exam's speaking and writing tasks.
- **New vocab:** L5 set (small by design)
- **Required visuals:** three-panel story arc (PROŠLOST 〰️• | SADAŠNJOST 📍 | BUDUĆNOST 🎯) with sentence frames per panel; A2 victory-lap card (the 7-case table complete, the clitic train, the aspect timeline — everything on one slide)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Sixteen units ago you couldn't read »dobar dan«. Today you tell your life story in Croatian." → Teach: three-panel arc + frames → Examples + TTS: *Planiram naučiti hrvatski do ljeta.* (I plan to learn Croatian by the summer.) · *Moj san je živjeti u Hrvatskoj.* (My dream is to live in Croatia.) · *Sljedeće godine preselit ću se u Split.* (Next year I'll move to Split.) → fill: guided my-story builder (7 blanks across the three panels) → type ×3 (one sentence per panel, own content) → listen-type ×2 (*Moj san je živjeti u Hrvatskoj.* · *Planiram naučiti hrvatski do ljeta.*) → speak: tell your story in 5+ sentences (recorded, self-assess vs rubric preview) → integration ×2 (Tom tells HIS story — the course's own arc) → unit-review slide set (8 quiz slides sampling L1–L4 + a taste of U13–15) → Recap + checkpoint-exam pointer
- **Interleaving:** comparatives (U13: *Život u Hrvatskoj je ljepši.*), dates (U14: *sljedeće godine*), clitics + future (U15/U9: *Javit ću vam se iz Splita.*)
- **SRS:** L5 vocab; sjećati se + GEN cloze (*Sjećam se ___ (djetinjstvo)* → djetinjstva); the learner's own three-panel sentences as personalized cards

## Story — «Priča bake Marije» (Grandma Marija's story)

Over coffee, baka Marija tells Tom her life: born in a small Slavonian village, grew up in a big family, worked in a factory, met her husband at a dance, moved, raised children — a chain of clean perfective milestones over imperfective decades. Then she turns the question around: »A ti, Tome? Što ćeš ti?« Tom answers with his plans — he'll keep learning Croatian, and next year he's moving to Croatia for good. 150–300 words; perfekt with contrastive aspect + future I; the whole recurring cast's backstory clicks into place.
Cast: baka Marija, Tom (Ana and Marko appear in baka's telling). New glossed words (≤5): unuk (grandson), mladić (young man), tvornica (factory), zauvijek (forever).
Sample sentences: »Rodila sam se u malom selu u Slavoniji«, počinje baka. »Radila sam u tvornici trideset godina.« · »Preselit ću se u Hrvatsku«, govori Tom. »Ovdje je moj život.«
Comprehension: 5 MC questions (Gdje se baka rodila? Koliko dugo je radila u tvornici? Što Tom planira? …).

## Unit test blueprint (17–19 items, pass ≥ 70%)

1. **Vocab (5):** match impf ↔ pf pairs ×2, MC ×3 (life events, plans words)
2. **Grammar (7):** aspect-in-past MC ×2 (context → pisao/napisao), aspect-in-future MC ×2 (radit ću/napravit ću), fill ×2 (*Baka je ___ (odrasti) u Slavoniji.*; *___ ću ti sutra (odgovoriti).*), pair fill ×1 (čitati → pf ___)
3. **Listening (4):** listen-type ×2 (*Rodio sam se u Kanadi.*, *Napravit ću plan.*), listen + MC ×2 (hear a sentence, was the action finished?)
4. **Reading (3):** a 5-line mini-biography + 3 MC comprehension
Example item: MC — »Jučer sam ___ knjigu cijeli dan.« (čitao / pročitao) → čitao · listen + MC — TTS: *Napisala je pismo.* "Is the letter finished?" → yes.
