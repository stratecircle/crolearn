# A2 Unit 11 — Zdravlje (Health & Body)

## Overview

No new case — this unit spends its budget on three constructions that make Croatian feel Croatian: **boli me + NOM** (the body part is the subject!), the four **modals** (morati, moći, smjeti, trebati + infinitive), and **DAT experiencer** chunks (loše mi je, treba mi), which extend U10's dative from receiver to feeler. Together they unlock the doctor's office — a top-3 survival scenario — and set up U15's clitic system with heavily drilled *me/mi* chunks.

## Can-do objectives

- I can say what hurts (Boli me glava; Bole me leđa)
- I can say what I must, can, may, and need to do
- I can say how I feel (Loše mi je; Muka mi je) and what I need (Treba mi odmor)
- I can describe symptoms to a doctor and understand simple instructions
- I can talk about healthy habits — sleep, exercise, food

## New vocabulary (~40 items)

**L1 (body & hurting):** glava (f, head) · ruka (f, arm/hand) · noga (f, leg/foot) · oko (n, eye; pl **oči**) · uho (n, ear; pl **uši**) · zub (m, tooth) · grlo (n, throat) · trbuh (m, stomach/belly) · leđa (n pl, back — plural-only!) · srce (n, heart) · boljeti (boli/bole, to hurt)

## Lessons

### L1 — Boli me glava (boli me + NOM)

- **Grammar micro-point:** **boli me + NOM** — the body part is the grammatical SUBJECT (nominative, blue!), the sufferer is a fixed ACC chunk *me/te*; verb agrees with the body part: *Boli me glava* / *Bole me leđa* (pl). Third persons use full ACC nouns: *Anu boli glava*, *Toma boli zub* (ga/ju wait for U15). → grammar-reference §3, §5
- **New vocab:** L1 set
- **Required visuals:** labelled body diagram (tap a part → TTS + word); **subject-flip diagram** for boli (EN "my head hurts ME?" → HR *glava* NOM-blue subject, *me* red ACC chunk — mirror of U10's sviđa-mi-se card); boli/bole agreement strip (sg part → boli, pl part → bole, with oči/uši/leđa flagged)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS groan-dialogue — »Što te boli?« »Boli me glava…« → Teach: subject-flip + body diagram → Examples + TTS: *Boli me glava.* (My head hurts.) · *Bole me leđa.* (My back hurts.) · *Toma boli zub.* (Tom's tooth hurts.) → MC ×3 (boli or bole?) → Teach: body vocab on the diagram → match ×1 (body part ↔ picture) → fill ×3 (*___ me grlo. — Boli*) → type ×2 → listen-type ×2 (*Bole me oči.*) → speak: answer »Što te boli?« → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** perfekt from U8 (*Jučer me je boljela glava* — je last, U8 clitic rule extended); ruka/noga sibilarization callback to U10 (ruci, nozi); imati from U3 (*Imam temperaturu* preview by TTS)
- **SRS:** L1 vocab (body diagram cards); cloze *___ me leđa. (Bole)*; cloze *Boli ___ glava. (me)*; irregular-plural cards oko→oči, uho→uši

### L2 — Moram ležati (Modals + infinitive)

- **Grammar micro-point:** the modal four + infinitive: **morati** (must/have to), **moći** (can — irregular: mogu, možeš… mogu), **smjeti** (may/be allowed), **trebati** (need/should) — *Moram ležati. Ne smiješ piti kavu.* Negation semantics: **ne moraš** = don't have to ≠ **ne smiješ** = mustn't. → grammar-reference §6 (moći), §7
- **New vocab:** L2 set
- **Required visuals:** modal ladder diagram (obligation scale: moraš → trebaš → možeš → smiješ, each rung with an example); moći conjugation table (mogu…mogu endpoints highlighted); ⚠️ contrast card *ne moraš* vs *ne smiješ* (traffic-light colors)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Doctor's orders — four verbs run every instruction." → Teach: modal + infinitive pattern (one verb conjugates, the other freezes) → Examples + TTS: *Moram ležati.* (I have to lie down.) · *Ne smiješ piti kavu.* (You mustn't drink coffee.) · *Možete li doći sutra?* (Can you come tomorrow?) → MC ×3 (pick the modal by meaning) → Teach: moći table + ne moraš/ne smiješ card → fill ×3 → type ×2 ("I can't sleep" → *Ne mogu spavati.*) → reorder ×1 → listen-type ×2 → speak: one thing you must do today → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** kava from U1/U4 (*Ne smiješ piti kavu*); spavati from U6; htjeti from U4 (želim/hoću vs moram meaning row)
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; moći conjugation clozes (mogu/možeš/mogu); contrast cloze *You don't have to = ne ___ (moraš)* vs *You mustn't = ne ___ (smiješ)*

### L3 — Loše mi je (DAT experiencer)

- **Grammar micro-point:** feelings land in the dative: **loše mi je, muka mi je, hladno mi je, dosadno mi je** and **treba mi + NOM** (*Treba mi odmor* — the needed thing is the subject, mirroring sviđa mi se). Third persons with full DAT nouns: *Ani je loše. Tomu treba lijek.* Chunks only (*mi/ti*) — system in U15. → grammar-reference §3, §5, §14
- **New vocab:** L3 set
- **Required visuals:** experiencer thermometer diagram (dobro mi je → loše mi je → muka mi je, face icons, DAT-orange *mi* constant); treba-mi flip card (needed thing NOM-blue, needer DAT-orange — third panel in the sviđa/boli family); symptom icon grid (prehlada 🤧, kašalj, temperatura 🌡️)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "You already say *drago mi je* — today you unlock the whole family." → Teach: experiencer pattern + thermometer → Examples + TTS: *Loše mi je i imam temperaturu.* (I feel bad and have a fever.) · *Treba mi odmor.* (I need a rest.) · *Ani je muka.* (Ana feels sick.) → MC ×3 (who feels what?) → Teach: symptom vocab grid → fill ×3 (*___ mi je. — Loše*) → type ×2 ("I need help" → *Treba mi pomoć.*) → listen-type ×2 → speak: answer *Kako si danas?* with an experiencer chunk (*Dobro mi je.* / *Loše mi je.*) → integration: mini-dialogue Tom↔Ana (fill) → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** drago mi je (U1) and sviđa mi se (U10) lined up beside loše mi je — one pattern, three units; vrijeme/hladno from U8 (*Hladno mi je* vs *Hladno je*); imati + ACC from U3 (*Imam prehladu*)
- **SRS:** L3 vocab; cloze *Treba ___ odmor. (mi)*; cloze *Ani ___ loše. (je)*; chunk cards for loše/muka/hladno mi je

### L4 — Kod liječnika (At the doctor's)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new machinery — the doctor-visit script assembled from U9–U11 parts: kod liječnika (GEN, U9), idem liječniku / k liječniku (DAT of goal, U10), modals for instructions (*Morate uzeti lijek* — imperatives wait for U14), boli-me anamnesis. → grammar-reference §14
- **New vocab:** L4 set
- **Required visuals:** doctor-visit flow diagram (čekaonica → pregled → recept → ljekarna, arrows with key phrase per station); realia card: a prescription-box label (*2× na dan, poslije jela*) as reading slide; kod vs k contrast mini-card (kod liječnika = at, k liječniku = to)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: full TTS consultation — "in 15 minutes you can do your half." → Teach: flow diagram + station phrases → Examples + TTS: *Idem liječniku jer me boli grlo.* (I'm going to the doctor because my throat hurts.) · *Morate uzeti tabletu dva puta na dan.* (You must take a pill twice a day.) · *Trebate ležati i piti puno vode.* (You need to lie down and drink a lot of water.) → MC ×3 (comprehension of instructions) → Teach: L4 vocab → fill ×4 (consultation dialogue blanks, learner plays patient) → type ×2 → listen-type ×2 (slow + normal) → speak: describe two symptoms out loud → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** puno vode GEN quantity from U7; jer-clauses from A1 connectors; U9 GEN preps (kod liječnika, bez recepta — *Ne mogu kupiti lijek bez recepta.*)
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; chunk cloze *dva puta ___ dan (na)*; cloze *Ne mogu kupiti lijek bez ___ . (recepta)*

### L5 — Zdrave navike (Healthy habits; unit review)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new grammar — habits in present + frequency adverbs (U6), obligations with modals, plans with future I (*Vježbat ću svaki dan!*). brinuti se o + LOC (*Brinem se o zdravlju*) as a chunk that recycles LOC. → grammar-reference §7, §9, §14
- **New vocab:** L5 set
- **Required visuals:** healthy-week planner grid (days × habits, checkmarks — doubles as speaking prompt); dobro/loše habit sort board (drag items: dovoljno spavati ✅, piti puno kave ❌); unit-recap strip (boli me · modals · mi-je chunks on one card)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Baka Marija's rules for a long life — do you agree?" → Teach: habit vocab + planner → Examples + TTS: *Trčim svaki dan i dovoljno spavam.* (I run every day and sleep enough.) · *Moraš se brinuti o zdravlju.* (You have to take care of your health.) · *Od sutra ću vježbati!* (From tomorrow I'll exercise!) → sort ×1 (healthy/unhealthy drag) → fill ×3 → type ×2 → integration ×3: baka Marija's advice to Tom (story-world quiz slides with modals) → listen-type ×2 → speak: two healthy-habit resolutions in future I → unit-review slide set (8 quiz slides sampling L1–L4) → Recap
- **Interleaving:** frequency adverbs from U6 (uvijek, često, nikad); od + GEN from U9 (*od sutra*); future I from U9 (*vježbat ću*)
- **SRS:** L5 vocab; cloze *Brinem se ___ zdravlju. (o)*; cloze *Od sutra ___ vježbati. (ću)*

## Story — «Tom je bolestan» (Tom is ill)

Tom wakes up ill: *Boli me glava, boli me grlo, loše mi je.* Ana takes him to the liječnica, who examines him — prehlada and temperatura; he must rest, may not drink coffee (tragedy!), and needs tea and sleep. Ana buys the lijek at the ljekarna, and baka Marija phones with her own prescription: chamomile tea with honey. In three days Tom is zdrav and celebrates — with one small coffee. 150–300 words; present, perfekt, future I, modals; NOM/ACC/LOC/GEN/DAT sg + taught chunks.
Cast: Tom, Ana, baka Marija (plus the liječnica as an unnamed role). New glossed words (≤5): kamilica (f, chamomile), med (m, honey), šalica (f, cup), odmah (right away).
Sample sentences: »Boli me glava i grlo, a imam i temperaturu.« · »Moraš ležati i ne smiješ piti kavu — treba ti san!«
Comprehension: 5 MC questions (Što Toma boli? Što Tom ne smije piti? Tko kupuje lijek? Što baka Marija savjetuje? — *savjetovati* appears only in the question stem, EN-glossed in UI).

## Unit test blueprint (17–19 items, pass ≥ 70%)

1. **Vocab (5):** body-diagram tap-match ×2, MC ×3 (symptom/pharmacy words)
2. **Grammar (7):** boli/bole fill ×2 (*___ me oči.*), modal MC ×2 (meaning: ne moraš vs ne smiješ), modal + infinitive fill ×1 (*Moram ___ (ležati)*), DAT experiencer fill ×2 (*Treba ___ odmor.* / *Ani ___ loše.*)
3. **Listening (4):** listen-type ×2 (TTS: *Boli me trbuh.*), listen + MC ×2 (doctor's instruction → what must the patient do?)
4. **Reading (3):** medicine-label + 2-line doctor note, 3 MC comprehension
Example item: MC — »Bolestan si. ___ ići na posao.« (a. Ne moraš b. Ne smiješ c. Nećeš — key: b) · listen-type — TTS says *glavobolja*, learner types it.
