# A2 Unit 9 — Na putu (Travel)

## Overview

The gateway to A2 and the first new tense since the perfekt: **future I** (htjeti-clitic + infinitive) lets the learner narrate forward for the first time — past (U8), present (U4–U6), and now future. The second half widens the GEN singular from U7's quantities into its **preposition system** (iz, do, od, kod, bez, blizu), which powers all travel talk: from where, to where, at whose place, without what. No new case this unit — DAT waits for Unit 10.

## Can-do objectives

- I can say what I will do tomorrow, next week, and this summer
- I can ask and answer questions about future plans (Hoćeš li…? Neću…)
- I can say where I'm travelling from, to, and near what (iz Zagreba, do Splita, blizu mora)
- I can buy a ticket, book a room, and check into a hotel
- I can plan a simple trip out loud, start to finish

## Lessons

### L1 — Sutra ću putovati (Future I: ću/ćeš + infinitive)

- **Grammar micro-point:** future I = htjeti clitic (ću ćeš će ćemo ćete će) + infinitive; clitic is second-position; spelling: verb-first drops -i of -ti (**pisat ću**, putovat ću) but -ći verbs keep everything (**doći ću**, ići ću); clitic-first order keeps full infinitive (Ja ću putovati / Sutra ću putovati). → grammar-reference §6 (htjeti), §9
- **New vocab:** L1 set
- **Required visuals:** htjeti-clitic conjugation table (6 persons, endings color-bolded); **future-I clitic placement card** — before→after transformation showing the clitic sliding to second position (Putovat **ću**. ↔ Sutra **ću** putovati. ↔ ~~Ću putovati~~ ❌) with the -t/-ći spelling rule as a two-column mini-table (pisati → pisat ću · doći → doći ću)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "You can already say yesterday (U8). After today: tomorrow." → Teach: ću-table + "same second-position rule as *sam* from U8" → Teach: the spelling card → Examples + TTS: *Sutra ću putovati u Split.* (Tomorrow I'll travel to Split.) · *Posjetit ću baku Mariju.* (I'll visit grandma Marija.) · *Doći ću u hotel u osam sati.* (I'll arrive at the hotel at eight.) → MC ×3 (pick the correct clitic; pick pisat ću vs pisati ću) → Teach: L1 vocab grid (beach/sea icons) → fill ×3 (word bank: ću/ćeš/će) → reorder ×2: sutra / ću / planirati / put → type ×2: "We will visit the island" → listen-type ×2 → speak: say one plan for tomorrow → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** perfekt contrast from U8 (*Jučer sam radio → Sutra ću raditi*); kava (U1) and kolodvor (U5) inside future sentences
- **SRS:** L1 vocab; cloze cards for all 6 future clitics; cloze pair "pisati → ___ ću (pisat)" / "doći → ___ ću (doći)"

### L2 — Hoćeš li doći? (Future I: questions & negation)

- **Grammar micro-point:** future questions with the FULL form + li (Hoću li? Hoćeš li doći?); negation with neću/nećeš… + infinitive (neću putovati); short answers (Hoću. / Neću.). → grammar-reference §6, §9, §15
- **New vocab:** L2 set
- **Required visuals:** three-row transformation card (statement → question → negation: *Putovat ćeš. → Hoćeš li putovati? → Nećeš putovati.*) with moving-word highlight; hoću/neću mirror table (affirmative vs negative, 6 persons)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS dialogue at a ticket counter — "this is you by the end." → Teach: Hoćeš li + inf → Examples + TTS: *Hoćeš li rezervirati sobu?* (Will you book a room?) · *Neću putovati sutra; putovat ću u subotu.* (I won't travel tomorrow; I'll travel on Saturday.) · *Hoće li Ana doći na kolodvor?* (Will Ana come to the station?) → MC ×3 → Teach: L2 vocab (timetable board visual: POLAZAK/DOLAZAK) → fill ×3: dialogue »Hoćete li kupiti povratnu kartu?« — »Hoću.« → type ×2 (produce a negation) → listen-type ×2 (TTS: *Vlak polazi u devet sati.*) → reorder ×1 → speak: answer "Hoćeš li putovati na more?" → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** subota/ponedjeljak (U6); Koliko košta karta? (U7); jesi li pattern from U2 (same li mechanics)
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; cloze "___ li doći? (Hoćeš)"; cloze "ne + future = ___ (neću)"

### L3 — Iz Zagreba do Splita (GEN prepositions)

- **Grammar micro-point:** the six-preposition GEN system: **iz** (from/out of), **do** (to/as far as), **od** (from), **kod** (at sb's place), **bez** (without), **blizu** (near) — all + GEN singular, endings already known from U7. Two are genuinely new (**kod**, **bez**); iz/od/do/blizu were introduced in U7 L2 and are consolidated here into the full travel system. → grammar-reference §3, §14
- **New vocab:** L3 set
- **Required visuals:** **GEN preposition usage-map diagram** (green): six boxes with arrows to example phrases (iz → *iz Zagreba*, kod → *kod bake*, bez → *bez putovnice*…); route arrow-line "iz Zagreba —— do Splita" over a mini map; GEN sg endings refresher strip (m -a, f -e, n -a) color-coded green
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Six little words unlock every travel sentence." → Teach: usage map (link back: U7 gave you the endings, today you get the doors they open) → Examples + TTS: *Putujemo iz Zagreba do Splita.* (We're travelling from Zagreb to Split.) · *Hotel je blizu mora.* (The hotel is near the sea.) · *Ne putujem bez putovnice.* (I don't travel without a passport.) → MC ×3 (pick the preposition) → Teach: L3 vocab (suitcase/passport icons; carina/granica scene) → fill ×4: *Spavat ćemo kod bake Marije.* → type ×2 → listen-type ×2 → speak: say your route aloud (*Putujem iz Zagreba do Splita.*) → integration: future + GEN combo (*Krenut ćemo iz hotela…* — preview krenuti by TTS only) → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** GEN quantities from U7 (čaša vode); grad/trg (U5); iz + countries callback to U2 (Ja sam iz Kanade)
- **SRS:** L3 vocab; cloze cards one per preposition (*Hotel je ___ mora. (blizu)*); production card "at grandma's = kod bake"

### L4 — Vlakom ili avionom? Ne — vlak ili avion! (Travel phrases; putovati & letjeti)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new machinery — consolidation lesson. Conjugation of putovati (putujem — -ovati→-ujem like kupovati U7) and letjeti (letim); high-frequency travel chunks (Jednu kartu do…, molim. · Imate li sobu? · Gdje je recepcija?). "How you travel" (by train/plane) is deliberately deferred to U12 INS — here transport is subject/object only. → grammar-reference §7
- **New vocab:** L4 set
- **Required visuals:** putovati + letjeti conjugation tables (endings bolded); hotel check-in flow diagram (recepcija → ključ → soba); transport icon grid (vlak 🚆, avion ✈️, zračna luka)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: full check-in dialogue via TTS. → Teach: putujem/letim tables → Examples + TTS: *Letimo u Dubrovnik u petak.* (We fly to Dubrovnik on Friday.) · *Jednu kartu do Splita, molim.* (One ticket to Split, please.) · *Imate li sobu za dvije noći?* (Do you have a room for two nights?) → MC ×3 → Teach: hotel vocab + flow diagram → fill ×3 (check-in dialogue blanks) → type ×2 → listen-type ×2 (normal + slow: *Zračna luka je blizu grada.*) → speak: order a ticket out loud → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** molim/izvolite/hvala café script from U1 reused at the counter; brojevi/prices from U7 (Karta košta deset eura); u/na + ACC direction from U5 (u Dubrovnik, na otok)
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; chunk cloze "Jednu kartu ___ Splita, molim. (do)"; putujem/letim conjugation clozes

### L5 — Planiramo put (Planning a trip; unit review)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new grammar — integrate future I + GEN prepositions into a full trip plan (spakirati → krenuti → stići). Verbs krenuti (krenem) and stići (stignem; perfekt stigao/stigla) presented as chunks with their future forms (krenut ću, stići ću). → grammar-reference §9
- **New vocab:** L5 set
- **Required visuals:** trip-timeline diagram (spakirat ću → krenut ćemo → stići ćemo, left-to-right arrow with clock icons); unit-recap grid (future forms + 6 GEN prepositions in one card)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Plan a whole trip in Croatian — out loud." → Teach: timeline verbs → Examples + TTS: *Spakirat ću kovčeg večeras.* (I'll pack the suitcase tonight.) · *Krenut ćemo rano ujutro.* (We'll set off early in the morning.) · *Stići ćemo u Split u podne.* (We'll arrive in Split at noon.) → fill ×3 (timeline story blanks) → reorder ×2 (ćemo / krenuti / sutra / iz / hotela) → integration ×3: Tom & Ana plan the story trip (quiz slides seeded with story sentences) → listen-type ×2 → speak: your own 3-sentence trip plan → unit-review slide set (8 quiz slides sampling L1–L4) → Recap
- **Interleaving:** telling time from U6 (u podne, u osam sati); vikend/jučer from U8 (past vs future contrast); restoran (U4)
- **SRS:** L5 vocab; cloze "krenuti → ___ ćemo (krenut)"; grammar card: one full model sentence per lesson of the unit

## Story — «Put na more» (A trip to the sea)

Tom and Ana plan a summer trip: they'll travel from Zagreb to Split, stay near the sea, and visit Marko, who now works in a restaurant near the beach. They buy tickets, pack (Tom almost forgets his passport — Ana: *Bez putovnice ne putuješ!*), and on the last evening baka Marija calls to hear the plan. (No modals — moći arrives in U11.) Told mostly in future I with a perfekt frame at the start. 150–300 words, present + perfekt + future I, cases: NOM/ACC/LOC/GEN sg only.
Cast: Tom, Ana, Marko (voice cameo: baka Marija by phone). New glossed words (≤5): trajekt (m, ferry), luka (f, harbour), uzbuđen (excited), sunčan (sunny).
Sample sentences: »Sutra ćemo putovati na more!« — »Hoćeš li rezervirati sobu blizu plaže?« · »Krenut ćemo rano iz Zagreba i stići ćemo u Split u podne.«
Comprehension: 5 MC questions (Kamo će Tom i Ana putovati? Gdje će spavati? Tko radi blizu plaže? …).

## Unit test blueprint (16–18 items, pass ≥ 70%)

1. **Vocab (5):** match HR↔EN ×2 (tickets/hotel sets), MC ×3 (picture/definition → word)
2. **Grammar (6):** future clitic fill ×2 (*Sutra ___ putovati. — ću*), spelling MC ×1 (pisat ću / pisati ću / pišat ću), question transform ×1 (reorder: li / doći / hoćeš), GEN preposition fill ×2 (*Putujem ___ Zagreba ___ Splita.*)
3. **Listening (4):** listen-type ×2 (TTS: *Vlak polazi u devet sati.*), listen + MC meaning ×2 (announcement-style)
4. **Reading (3):** 4-line trip-plan micro-passage + 3 MC comprehension
Example item: fill — »Nećemo letjeti; ___ ćemo vlak. (rezervirat/kupit-type answer bank)« · listen-type — TTS says *putovnica*, learner types it.

## NotesDoc focus per lesson

- **L1:** full future-I paradigm incl. spelling table; mistakes: ~~Ću putovati~~ ❌ → *Putovat ću* ✅ (clitic never first — same law as *sam* U8), ~~pisati ću~~ ❌ spelling → *pisat ću* ✅ but *doći ću* keeps -ći, translating "going to" with ići ❌; hook: "same train, new wagon — ću sits where sam sits"
- **L2:** hoću/neću mirror table; mistakes: ~~ne ću~~ ❌ (fused *neću* — one of only three fused negatives: nisam, neću, nemam), answering *Hoćeš li…?* with *Da* instead of *Hoću* (unnatural), ~~Hoćeš doći?~~ without li (colloquial — mark heard-but-not-taught)
- **L3:** the six prepositions with the usage map reproduced; mistakes: iz vs od (*iz Zagreba* = from inside a place, *od Ane* = from a person), kod = French "chez"/at sb's place (not "beside"), ~~bez putovnica~~ ❌ → *bez putovnice* ✅ (GEN, not NOM); hook: "green light = GEN after all six"
- **L4:** putovati→putujem pattern grouped with kupovati→kupujem (U7); mistake: ~~Putujem s vlakom~~ preview-flagged ❌ (bare INS comes in U12 — for now say *Idem u Split vlakom* is NOT yet available; use *Putujem u Split*); dialogue map for check-in
- **L5:** trip-timeline reproduced; common mistakes recap of the unit (clitic-first, pisati ću, bez + NOM); hook: past-present-future triangle — one sentence in all three (*Radio sam · Radim · Radit ću*)
