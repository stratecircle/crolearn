# A2 Unit 10 — Kod kuće (Home & Living)

## Overview

The fifth case enters the spine: **DAT singular**, the case of the receiver (dajem mami), of motion-toward with prema/k, and — via the fixed chunk *sviđa mi se* — of the liker. Endings are a gift: DAT sg is identical to LOC sg (U5), so the learner mostly re-labels forms they already own. The home/renting theme supplies concrete give-show-help situations, and L3's ACC/DAT contrast inoculates against the #1 anglophone error (giving the receiver in accusative).

## Can-do objectives

- I can say who I give, show, or send something to (Dajem baki poklon)
- I can say what I like and ask what someone likes (Sviđa mi se stan)
- I can name the rooms and furniture of a home and describe mine
- I can talk about chores — who cleans, washes, helps whom
- I can describe my living situation (renting, flatmates, moving)

## Lessons

### L1 — Dajem baki poklon (DAT singular: the receiver)

- **Grammar micro-point:** **DAT singular** — m/n **-u** (bratu, djetetu), f -a **-i** (mami, Ani, baki), f -i **-i** (noći); sibilarization k→c, g→z (majka→majci, knjiga→knjizi); use: the receiver of giving/showing/sending/telling; pomagati takes DAT (pomažem susjedu). → grammar-reference §3, §14
- **New vocab:** L1 set
- **Required visuals:** **DAT declension table** (color-coded orange endings, all three genders + the fem-i row) AND **DAT usage-map diagram** (orange boxes: give→komu?, show→komu?, help→komu?, toward→prema/k, liking→sviđa mi se [preview], each with an example phrase); giver→📦→receiver arrow diagram (subject NOM blue, object ACC red, receiver DAT orange — three-color sentence X-ray of *Tom daje Ani ključ*)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "One ending answers 'to whom?' — and you secretly know it already (it's the LOC ending from U5)." → Teach: DAT table + LOC-twin note → Teach: usage map → Examples + TTS (endings orange-highlighted): *Dajem mami poklon.* (I'm giving mum a present.) · *Tom pomaže susjedu.* (Tom helps the neighbour.) · *Poslala sam baki pismo.* (I sent grandma a letter.) → MC ×3 (pick the DAT form: Ani/Anu/Ane) → Teach: L1 vocab → fill ×3 → type ×2 ("I help Ana" → *Pomažem Ani.*) → sentence-X-ray drag ×1 (color the three roles) → listen-type ×2 → speak: say who you help at home (*Pomažem mami.* / *Pomažem bratu.*) → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** family nouns from U3 as receivers (mama, brat, sestra, baka); poklon + kupiti from U7 (*Kupila sam bratu poklon.*); LOC forms from U5 (same shape, different job)
- **SRS:** L1 vocab; cloze cards *Dajem ___ poklon. (mami)* / *Pomažem ___ . (bratu)*; sibilarization cloze *majka → dajem ___ (majci)*

### L2 — Sviđa mi se! (DAT prepositions prema/k; sviđati se)

- **Grammar micro-point:** DAT prepositions **prema** (towards) and **k/ka** (to sb's place/toward a person): *prema gradu*, *k baki*; plus the chunk-of-the-course **sviđa mi se + NOM** — the liked thing is the SUBJECT, the liker is DAT (*Sviđa mi se stan* / *Ani se sviđa balkon*). Only *mi/ti* chunks here — the full clitic system waits for U15. → grammar-reference §5, §12, §14
- **New vocab:** L2 set
- **Required visuals:** **subject-flip diagram** for sviđati se (EN "I ❤ the flat" → HR *stan* NOM-blue is the subject, *mi* DAT-orange is the liker, arrow flip animation); prema/k arrow diagram (person walking prema zgradi / k susjedi); vrata warning card (⚠️ plural-only noun: *Vrata su velika.*)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "In Croatian you don't like things — things are pleasing to you." → Teach: subject-flip diagram (*Sviđa mi se stan.* vs ~~Sviđam stan~~ ❌) → Examples + TTS: *Sviđa mi se tvoj stan.* (I like your flat.) · *Ani se sviđa balkon.* (Ana likes the balcony.) · *Idemo prema zgradi.* (We're walking towards the building.) → MC ×3 (who likes what? role-identification) → Teach: L2 vocab on a cutaway-building diagram (ulaz, kat, dizalo…) → fill ×3 (*Sviđa ___ se kuhinja. — mi*) → type ×2 → reorder ×1: se / mi / sviđa / zgrada → listen-type ×2 → speak: say one thing you like about your home → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** *drago mi je* from U1 unmasked ("you've used a DAT chunk since day 4!"); grad from U5 (*prema gradu*); adjective agreement from U2/U3 (*Vrata su velika* — n pl)
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; chunk cloze *___ mi se stan. (Sviđa)*; cloze *Idemo ___ gradu. (prema)*; cloze *k ___ (baki)*

### L3 — Tko kome pomaže? (Household verbs; ACC vs DAT contrast)

- **Grammar micro-point:** verb valence — čistiti/pospremati/prati take ACC (the thing acted on: *perem suđe*), pomagati takes DAT (the person helped: *pomažem baki*), davati/pokazivati take BOTH (thing ACC + receiver DAT). No new endings — role discipline. → grammar-reference §3, §14
- **New vocab:** L3 set
- **Required visuals:** verb-valence contrast table (red ACC column vs orange DAT column: čistim **što** · pomažem **komu** · dajem **komu što**); chore icon grid (suđe 🍽️, rublje 👕, smeće 🗑️); two-color sentence X-rays ×3
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Red or orange? Every verb has a color habit." → Teach: valence table → Examples + TTS: *Čistim kuhinju svaki dan.* (I clean the kitchen every day.) · *Marko pere suđe, a Ana pomaže susjedi.* (Marko washes the dishes and Ana helps the neighbour.) · *Tom daje cimeru ključ.* (Tom gives his flatmate the key.) → MC ×3 (ACC or DAT? pick the form) → Teach: chore vocab grid → fill ×4 (mixed ACC/DAT blanks) → type ×2 → match ×1 (verb ↔ case color) → listen-type ×2 → speak: name two chores you do → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** ACC drill objects from U4 (perem čašu, čistim stol — kava/pivo world); reflexive verb mechanics from U6 (tuširam se → pattern echo in pospremam); svaki dan frequency from U6
- **SRS:** L3 vocab; contrast cloze pair *Čistim ___ (kuhinju)* / *Pomažem ___ (mami)*; card "prati → perem" (irregular present)

### L4 — Gdje stanuješ? (Living situations)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new machinery — consolidation around stanovati (stanujem, -ovati→-ujem), iznajmiti, preseliti se (perfekt: *preselio se* — se, no je, from U8 L3 rule); kod + GEN for living at someone's (kod bake, kod brata) recycled from U9. → grammar-reference §7, §8
- **New vocab:** L4 set
- **Required visuals:** living-situations icon row (own flat 🏠 / renting 🔑 / s cimerom → phrased as *ima cimera* / kod roditelja → phrased *kod bake* until GEN pl in U13); mini rental-ad visual (STAN ZA NAJAM: stanarina, kat, balkon) used as a realia reading slide
- **Slide arc:** Hook: a real-style rental ad — "you'll read one and call the landlord." → Teach: stanujem/iznajmim + situations row → Examples + TTS: *Stanujem u stanu u centru grada.* (I live in a flat in the city centre.) · *Ana želi iznajmiti sobu.* (Ana wants to rent a room.) · *Marko se preselio u Split.* (Marko moved to Split.) → MC ×3 (ad-reading comprehension) → Teach: L4 vocab → fill ×3 (dialogue with vlasnik: *Kolika je stanarina?* presented as a fixed chunk) → type ×2 → listen-type ×2 (slow + normal: *Stan je na trećem katu.*) → reorder ×1 → speak: describe where you live (2 sentences) → interleave → Recap
- **Interleaving:** ordinals/floors from U5 (na trećem katu); prices from U7 (stanarina je petsto eura); perfekt + se word order from U8 (*preselio se*)
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; cloze *Marko ___ ___ u Split. (se preselio)*; chunk card "Kolika je stanarina?"

### L5 — Moj stan (My home; unit review)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new grammar — full home description integrating rooms (LOC), furniture (NOM/ACC), likes (sviđa mi se), and receivers (DAT). → grammar-reference §3, §14
- **New vocab:** L5 set
- **Required visuals:** labelled floor-plan diagram (each room clickable → TTS: kupaonica, hodnik, dnevni boravak…); furniture-in-room semantic grid; unit-recap card (DAT endings + prema/k + sviđa mi se in one strip)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: floor-plan tour narrated by TTS — "now walk someone through yours." → Teach: rooms on the floor plan → Examples + TTS: *U dnevnom boravku je velik stol.* (In the living room there's a big table.) · *Sviđa mi se spavaća soba.* (I like the bedroom.) · *Pokazat ću Ani novi stan.* (I'll show Ana the new flat.) → match ×1 (room ↔ furniture) → fill ×3 (floor-plan blanks) → type ×2 → integration ×3: Tom describes his flat to baka Marija (story-world quiz slides) → listen-type ×2 → speak: 3-sentence tour of your home → unit-review slide set (8 quiz slides sampling L1–L4) → Recap
- **Interleaving:** LOC location sentences from U5 (u kuhinji, na balkonu); future I from U9 (*Pokazat ću Ani…*); velik/malen adjectives from U3
- **SRS:** L5 vocab; cloze *U ___ je krevet. (spavaćoj sobi — accept "sobi")*; grammar card: one model DAT sentence per lesson

## Story — «Novi stan» (The new flat)

Ana finds a new flat near the faculty and Tom helps her move: he carries boxes, she shows him every room, and they argue sweetly about where the ormar should go. Baka Marija sends a package — a present for the new home — and Ana phones her: *Hvala ti, bako…* is NOT used (VOC waits for U14); instead the narration has *Ana je poslala baki poruku.* Everything they like gets a *sviđa mi se*. 150–300 words; present + perfekt + future I; NOM/ACC/LOC/GEN/DAT sg.
Cast: Tom, Ana, baka Marija. New glossed words (≤5): kutija (f, box), kauč (m, sofa), poruka (f, message), težak (heavy).
Sample sentences: »Pomažem Ani — sutra će se preseliti u novi stan.« · »Sviđa mi se dnevni boravak, ali kuhinja je mala.«
Comprehension: 5 MC questions (Komu Tom pomaže? Što je baka Marija poslala Ani? Koja soba se Tomu sviđa? …).

## Unit test blueprint (16–18 items, pass ≥ 70%)

1. **Vocab (5):** match HR↔EN ×2 (rooms; chores), MC ×3 (floor-plan picture → word)
2. **Grammar (6):** DAT fill ×3 (*Dajem ___ poklon. — sestri/bratu/baki*), ACC-vs-DAT MC ×2 (*Pomažem susjeda/susjedu?*), sviđa-mi-se reorder ×1 (se / mi / sviđa / stan)
3. **Listening (4):** listen-type ×2 (TTS: *Perem suđe u kuhinji.*), listen + MC ×2 (which room is described?)
4. **Reading (3):** rental-ad micro-passage + 3 MC comprehension
Example item: MC — »Tom daje ključ ___.« (a. cimera b. cimeru c. cimer) · listen-type — TTS says *kupaonica*, learner types it.

- **L3:** the valence table as the centrepiece; mistakes: case-blind translation of "give her the key" (her = *joj*? no — full noun until U15: *Dajem Ani ključ*), ~~perem suđu~~ ❌ (suđe is n ACC=NOM); memory hook: red = the thing touched, orange = the person served
- **L4:** stanovati vs živjeti nuance note (both fine; stanovati = reside); mistakes: ~~Stanujem u Zagreb~~ ❌ → *u Zagrebu* ✅ (LOC after location u — U5 rule still alive), perfekt ~~preselio se je~~ ❌ → *preselio se* ✅ (se eats je, U8 L3); chunk *Kolika je stanarina?* memorized whole
- **L5:** floor plan + furniture tables complete; mistakes: vrata treated as singular (~~Vrata je velika~~ ❌ → *Vrata su velika* ✅), room names with wrong u/na (na balkonu but u kuhinji); povezano: forward link — "in U12 you'll say WHAT YOU CLEAN WITH (krpom, usisavačem — instrumental)"
