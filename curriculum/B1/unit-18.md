# B1 Unit 18 — Obrazovanje (Education & learning) · Conditional I

## Overview

The politeness unit, disguised as a grammar unit. Conditional I is the single cheapest piece of machinery left in the language — **it is the perfekt participle you have used since Unit 8 with a different clitic in front of it** (`sam htio` → `bih htio`) — and it unlocks four things at once that a B1 learner uses every day: asking for something without sounding blunt (*Htio bih…*, *Biste li mogli…?*), saying what you wish were true (*Da imam vremena…*), reasoning about what isn't (*Kad bih imao novca, studirao bih u Zagrebu.*) and giving advice (*Na tvom mjestu bih…*, *Trebalo bi…*). The education theme carries it because school is where Croatians ask, wish, hypothesize and advise most: which course to enrol in, which exam to sit, what you'd study if you started again. L3 also finally cards **ako** — untaught until now — so the learner can feel the difference between a condition that might happen (*Ako imam vremena, doći ću.*) and one that won't (*Da imam vremena, došao bih.*). Unit shape: 5 lessons + 300–500-word story + unit test + guided writing task (80–150 words, AI rubric-graded, never gates).

## Can-do objectives

- I can form Conditional I for any verb I know: bih/bi/bi/bismo/biste/bi + the same l-participle I use for the past, agreeing for gender and number
- I can ask for things politely at three levels of register (Daj mi… → Možeš li mi dati…? → Biste li mi mogli dati…?) and choose the right one for ti or Vi
- I can distinguish a real condition (ako + present → future I) from an unreal one (kad bih / da + present → conditional), and not mix their halves
- I can give advice with the four standard frames: Na tvom mjestu bih…, Trebao/Trebala bi + inf, Trebalo bi + inf, Mogao/Mogla bi + inf
- I can write 80–150 connected words about what I would study and why, using conditional in at least three of its four functions

## Lessons

### L1 — U školi i na fakultetu (Conditional I: formation & the clitic slot)

- **Grammar micro-point:** conditional I is **the participle you already own** + a new clitic set: **bih, bi, bi, bismo, biste, bi** + l-participle (*htio bih, htjela bi, mogli bismo*). Nothing about the participle changes — same forms as perfekt, same gender/number agreement (htio/htjela, radili/radile). Three consequences to teach explicitly: (a) the clitic parks in the **aux slot** of the second-position cluster, exactly where sam/ću sit — *Ja bih to napisao.* / *Napisao bih to.* / *Htio bih vam nešto reći.*; (b) **2sg and 3sg are both `bi`** — the pronoun or context does the disambiguating (*Ti bi rekao* vs *On bi rekao*); (c) negation is **ne bih** (two words, never *nebih*), question is **Bi li…? / Biste li…?**. → grammar-reference §10 "Conditional I" + §12 clitic order
- **New vocab:** L1 set (school-institution words)
- **Required visuals:** **flow diagram — the participle swap** ([perfekt: `sam` + htio] → swap the clitic → [conditional: `bih` + htio], with a third box showing the participle is byte-identical) · **train diagram** (the U15 clitic train redrawn with the `bih bi bismo biste` wagon highlighted in the aux slot) · conjugation table bih/bi/bi/bismo/biste/bi with a **bi ×2** warning column · school-vs-university vocab table (osnovna/srednja škola · učenik · razred ‖ sveučilište · fakultet · student)
- **Slide arc:** menu → warm-up quiz-set (U8 participle agreement ×2 + U15 clitic position ×1) → Hook: "Back in Unit 15 you saw a word in the clitic train you had never used: **bih**. Today you cash it in — and it costs you nothing, because you already know the hard half." → vocab-cards (L1 set) → Teach: participle-swap flow + conjugation table + the bi/bi collision → Examples + TTS: *Htio bih upisati fakultet.* (I'd like to enrol at the faculty.) · *Ana bi studirala u Zagrebu.* (Ana would study in Zagreb.) · *Mi bismo rado pomogli.* → **CORRECTION: rado/pomoći are untaught** → *Mi bismo to naučili.* (We would learn that.) · *Ne bih to rekao.* (I wouldn't say that.) → quiz-set ×4 (pick the clitic for the subject; spot the wrong participle agreement) → match (pronoun ↔ conditional clitic) → fill ×3 (*Ja ___ studirao u Zagrebu.* → bih) → type ×2 → listen-type ×2 (*Htio bih upisati fakultet.* · *Ne bih to rekao.*) → speak (two things you would do) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U8 perfekt participle agreement (the whole lesson leans on it), U15 clitic second-position, U12 studirati / fakultet in a school sentence
- **SRS:** L1 vocab; conjugation card "mi + conditional → ___" → bismo; cloze "Ana ___ studirala u Zagrebu." → bi; form card "negative of bih → ___" → ne bih
- **Note:** `bi` for 2sg/3sg is the highest-value warning in the unit — every drill that uses 2sg or 3sg must supply an explicit pronoun or subject noun so the learner never has to guess.

### L2 — Molim Vas… (Polite requests, offers and wishes)

- **Grammar micro-point:** conditional's most common everyday job is **taking the edge off**. Four frames: **Htio bih / Htjela bih** + noun or infinitive (I'd like…), **Želio bih / Željela bih** + noun/inf (the slightly more formal twin), **Mogao bih / Mogla bih** + inf (I could — offering), and the request form **Bi li mogao…? / Biste li mogli…?** (Could you…?). The register ladder is the lesson's spine: **Daj mi bilježnicu.** (imperative, blunt, friends only) → **Možeš li mi dati bilježnicu?** (neutral, ti) → **Biste li mi mogli dati bilježnicu?** (formal, Vi). Clitic order inside these frames follows §12 unchanged: aux (`bih`) → DAT → ACC — *Htio bih vam objasniti raspored.* → grammar-reference §10 + §12
- **New vocab:** L2 set (enrolment & classroom-request words)
- **Required visuals:** **bracket diagram — the politeness ladder** (three rungs: Daj mi… / Možeš li mi…? / Biste li mi mogli…?, each labelled with who you say it to) · **callout — ti or Vi?** (Bi li…? = ti · Biste li…? = Vi; getting this wrong is the one mistake a Croatian actually notices) · request-frame table (frame | literal | when to use)
- **Slide arc:** menu → warm-up quiz-set (L1 clitics ×2 + U11 moći ×1) → Hook: "There is a way to ask for something that makes a stranger want to help you. It is one syllable long." → vocab-cards (L2 set) → Teach: politeness ladder + frame table + the ti/Vi callout → Examples + TTS: *Htio bih upisati tečaj hrvatskog.* (I'd like to enrol in a Croatian course.) · *Biste li mi mogli objasniti raspored?* (Could you explain the timetable to me?) · *Bi li mi ponovila tu rečenicu?* → **CORRECTION: rečenica is L4** → *Bi li mi ponovila pitanje?* (Would you repeat the question for me?) · *Željela bih vidjeti prijavu.* (I'd like to see the application form.) → quiz-set ×4 (which rung fits this person?) → mc ×2 (Bi li or Biste li for a professor? for a classmate?) → fill ×3 → type ×2 (turn a blunt imperative into a polite request) → listen-type ×2 (*Htio bih upisati tečaj hrvatskog.* · *Biste li mi mogli objasniti raspored?*) → reorder ×1 (clitic cluster: *Htio bih vam objasniti raspored.*) → speak (ask the university office for three things) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U15 DAT clitics inside the frames (mi/vam), U11 moći/trebati as the neutral middle rung, U2 formal Vi from the very first greetings lesson
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; cloze "___ li mi mogli objasniti raspored? (formal)" → Biste; chunk card "I'd like to enrol… → ___" → Htio/Htjela bih upisati…; cloze "Htio bih ___ objasniti raspored. (to you, formal)" → vam
- **Note:** *Htio bih* is masculine and *Htjela bih* feminine — the drills must accept both where the speaker is the learner, and the vocab card should show the pair, not one form.

### L3 — Da imam vremena… (Real vs unreal conditions)

- **Grammar micro-point:** the unit's conceptual centre. Croatian splits conditions in two, and the halves must not be mixed:
  - **REAL / open** — it might well happen: **ako + present** in the if-clause, **future I** in the main clause. *Ako imam vremena, doći ću.* (If I have time, I'll come.) Note the Croatian present where English uses a present too — no future in the ako-clause at this level.
  - **UNREAL / hypothetical** — it isn't so: either **kad bih + participle, … bih + participle** (*Kad bih imao vremena, došao bih.*) or **da + present, … bih + participle** (*Da imam vremena, došao bih.*). The two unreal patterns mean the same thing; `da` + present is the everyday spoken one.
  `da` here is the conjunction **"if"** — it is NOT the reported-speech `da` (U21) and NOT a purpose clause. Say so out loud in the teach slide. → grammar-reference §10; ako + Future II arrives at U22
- **New vocab:** L3 set (ako, kad + exam words)
- **Required visuals:** **contrast diagram — two roads** (left: REAL, ako + present → future I, drawn as a fork the walker can still take; right: UNREAL, kad bih / da + present → bih, drawn as a road with a barrier across it) · **flow diagram — the unreal pair** ([kad bih + participle] ⇄ [da + present] → same meaning → main clause always `bih` + participle) · **callout — never `ako` + `bih`** (\*Ako bih imao vremena ❌ is the single most common learner error here) · exam-vocabulary table (polagati = to be sitting it ‖ položiti = to pass it — an aspect pair, straight out of U17's machinery)
- **Slide arc:** menu → warm-up quiz-set (L2 frames ×2 + U17 aspect pair ×1) → Hook: "«If I have time» and «if I had time» are one word apart in English. In Croatian they are two different grammars — and the difference is whether it is going to happen." → vocab-cards (L3 set) → Teach 1: the two-roads contrast → Teach 2: the unreal pair (kad bih ⇄ da + present) + the ako-bih callout → Examples + TTS: *Ako imam vremena, doći ću.* (If I have time, I'll come.) · *Da imam vremena, došao bih.* (If I had time, I'd come.) · *Kad bih imao novca, upisao bih tečaj.* (If I had money, I'd enrol in the course.) · *Ako položim ispit, dobit ću dobru ocjenu.* → **CORRECTION: dobiti is untaught** → *Ako položim ispit, bit ću sretan.* (If I pass the exam, I'll be happy.) → quiz-set ×4 (real or unreal? which main clause?) → mc ×2 (*Ako ___ vremena…* imam / bih imao) → fill ×4 (complete both halves, both patterns) → reorder ×1 (a kad bih sentence) → type ×2 → listen-type ×2 (*Da imam vremena, došao bih.* · *Ako imam vremena, doći ću.*) → speak (one real plan + one impossible wish) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U9 future I in the real-condition main clause (doći ću vs pisat ću spelling, live), U17 polagati/položiti as a fresh aspect pair, U8 participle agreement inside the unreal halves
- **SRS:** L3 vocab; cloze "___ imam vremena, doći ću." → Ako; cloze "Da imam vremena, ___ bih." → došao/došla; pattern card "kad bih + ___" → l-participle; error card "\*Ako bih imao → ✅ ___" → Ako imam / Kad bih imao
- **Note:** `kad` is carded here in its conditional sense; its temporal sense ("when") has been in use since U8 and is treated on the card as a second gloss, not a second lesson. Future II in kad/ako-clauses (Kad budem imao vremena…) is explicitly deferred to U22 — the teach slide may name it as "coming later" but must not drill it.

### L4 — Učenje jezika (Advice: what you'd do in their place)

- **Grammar micro-point:** four advice frames, ordered from most personal to most impersonal:
  1. **Na tvom mjestu bih + participle** / **Na vašem mjestu bih…** — "in your place I'd…" (taught as a **fixed frame**; na + LOC of the possessive is not otherwise drilled here)
  2. **Trebao bi / Trebala bi + infinitive** — "you should", personal, the participle **agrees with the person advised**
  3. **Trebalo bi + infinitive** — "one ought to / it would be good to", impersonal, participle **always neuter singular**, no subject at all
  4. **Mogao bi / Mogla bi + infinitive** — "you could", the soft suggestion
  The trap worth a whole callout: **trebao bi** (agrees — *Ana, trebala bi ponoviti.*) vs **trebalo bi** (never agrees — *Trebalo bi ponoviti sve.*). Impersonal constructions as a system arrive at U20; here `trebalo bi` is a frame, not a paradigm. → grammar-reference §10; U11 trebati is the personal-verb baseline
- **New vocab:** L4 set (language-learning words)
- **Required visuals:** **usage-map diagram — four ways to say "you should"** (four branches from a central "advice" node, each with its frame, an example and a one-line "how it lands" note: warm / direct / neutral / gentle) · **callout — trebao bi vs trebalo bi** (agrees ‖ never agrees, with a wrong/right pair) · riječ i-declension mini-table (riječ, riječi, riječi — the f-noun that ends in a consonant) · razumjeti present table (razumijem, razumiješ, razumije, razumijemo, razumijete, razumiju)
- **Slide arc:** menu → warm-up quiz-set (L3 conditions ×2 + U11 trebati ×1) → Hook: "Croatian has four ways to tell someone what to do, and only one of them sounds like an order." → vocab-cards (L4 set) → Teach 1: the four-frame usage map → Teach 2: trebao bi / trebalo bi callout + razumjeti and riječ form tables → Examples + TTS: *Na tvom mjestu bih upisao tečaj.* (In your place I'd enrol in a course.) · *Trebala bi ponoviti te riječi.* (You should revise those words.) · *Trebalo bi vježbati svaki dan.* (One ought to practise every day.) · *Mogao bi zapamtiti pet riječi na dan.* (You could memorize five words a day.) · *Ne razumijem tu rečenicu.* (I don't understand that sentence.) → quiz-set ×4 (which frame is this? agrees or not?) → mc ×2 (trebao bi / trebalo bi) → match (frame ↔ how it lands) → fill ×3 → type ×2 (give a friend two pieces of advice) → listen-type ×2 (*Trebala bi ponoviti te riječi.* · *Trebalo bi vježbati svaki dan.*) → speak (advise a beginner on learning Croatian, 3+ sentences) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U11 trebati/moći as plain verbs (the contrast that makes the conditional frames feel softer), U13 adjectives for describing advice (dobar savjet, teška vježba), U17 ponoviti as a prefixed perfective (po- + noviti pattern, recognized)
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; cloze "Na tvom ___ bih upisao tečaj." → mjestu; error card "\*Trebalo bi ponoviti — ali Ana? → ___" → Ana bi trebala ponoviti / Trebala bi ponoviti; form card "I don't understand → ___" → Ne razumijem; form card "GEN of riječ → ___" → riječi
- **Note:** the lesson title uses **učenje**, a verbal noun. Verbal nouns are U23 — the word appears in the title and may appear once, glossed, in the teach body, but it is never carded and never quizzed.

### L5 — Moje obrazovanje (Consolidation + the writing task)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new machinery — **all four conditional functions interleaved** (wish · request · hypothesis · advice) in mixed contexts, plus the ako/da contrast under time pressure; then the unit's exit performance: the guided writing task *"Što bih studirao i zašto"* (80–150 words), rehearsed on-slide before the test carries it. → grammar-reference §10 (whole section)
- **New vocab:** L5 set (small by design)
- **Required visuals:** **bracket diagram — the conditional's four jobs** (one `bih` at the root, four branches: WISH *Htio bih…* · REQUEST *Biste li…?* · HYPOTHESIS *Da imam…, …bih* · ADVICE *Na tvom mjestu bih…* — the unit's printable) · my-education-path planner (osnovna škola → srednja škola → fakultet → zanimanje, each stage with one real fact and one *bih*-sentence) · **callout — writing scaffold** (opening: what you studied / are studying → 2× *bih* wish → 1× unreal condition with da or kad bih → 1× advice to your past self → closing plan in future I)
- **Slide arc:** menu → warm-up quiz-set (L4 advice frames ×2 + U16 budućnost ×1) → Hook: "Four jobs, one clitic. ## Story — «Tečaj u Zagrebu» (A course in Zagreb)

**~330 words (B1 length: 300–500).** Tom, in Canada, writes to Ana: he'd like to spend the summer in Zagreb and enrol in a Croatian course, but there are three of them and he can't choose. The story is a **chain of advice**, so every paragraph carries conditional in a different function. Ana answers with the frames from L4 (*Na tvom mjestu bih upisala ljetni tečaj — trebalo bi se prijaviti do lipnja.*). Marko disagrees warmly: he'd learn by talking to guests in the restaurant, not in a classroom (*Ja bih učio u restoranu!*). Baka Marija delivers the emotional beat — in her day there were no courses and no scholarships: *Da sam ja imala takvu priliku…* (her one **counterfactual-past** line, glossed as a chunk, NOT taught as Conditional II). Tom decides: he'd like the summer course, he'd like Ana to explain the application, and if he passes the entrance test he'll come in July. Closing line is a real condition in future I, so the story lands on something that will actually happen.

Cast: Tom, Ana, Marko, baka Marija.
New glossed words (≤5, all verified absent from `src/content` on 2026-07-29): **barem** (at least / if only), **ljetni** (summer-, adj), **prijaviti se** (to apply, sign up — built on the L2 noun prijava, so it reads as half-known), **odabrati** (pf, to choose; odabrao/odabrala), **savršen** (perfect).
Sample sentences: *Htio bih upisati tečaj hrvatskog u Zagrebu, ali ne znam koji.* · *Na tvom mjestu bih upisala ljetni tečaj — trebalo bi se prijaviti do lipnja.* · *Ja bih učio u restoranu, s gostima!* · *Ako položim ispit, doći ću u srpnju.*
**Authoring guards:** `koji` appears ONCE, in the fixed nominative question *…ali ne znam koji.* (nominative, undeclined — U19 declines it; the course already has *Koji je datum danas?* and *Koji kanal gledaš?*, so this is in-pattern); baka's *Da sam ja imala tu priliku…* is glossed inline as a set phrase and must NOT be echoed in the comprehension questions or drilled anywhere — it is Conditional-II-adjacent and belongs to B2; no `se`-passive; direct quotes only, never *rekla je da…*; **ispit**, not *test* (test is uncarded); months **lipanj / srpanj are taught** (a2u14 L4) and may be used freely; **iskustvo** (a2u12 L5) and **mladost** (a2u16 L2) are already carded — do NOT gloss them, use them plainly if useful.

## Unit test blueprint (18–20 items + writing, pass ≥ 70% on quiz sections)

1. **Vocab (5):** match education words ×1 (4 pairs: sveučilište / ocjena / vještina / stipendija), MC ×2 (učenik vs student — which school? ‖ polagati vs položiti — which one means you passed?), fill ×2 (*Napisao sam pet novih ___ u bilježnicu.* → riječi; *Dobio je odličnu ___ na ispitu.* → **CORRECTION: dobiti untaught** → *Ima odličnu ___ iz hrvatskog.* → ocjenu)
2. **Grammar (8):** conditional-form MC ×2 (*Mi ___ to naučili.* bismo/biste ‖ *Ana ___ studirala u Zagrebu.* bi/bih), real-vs-unreal MC ×2 (*Ako imam vremena, ___* doći ću / došao bih ‖ *Da imam vremena, ___* doći ću / došao bih), politeness-register MC ×1 (which form for a professor?), fill ×2 (*Na tvom ___ bih upisao tečaj.* → mjestu; *___ li mi mogli objasniti raspored?* → Biste), advice-frame type ×1 (turn *Trebalo bi ponoviti.* into advice aimed at Ana → *Ana bi trebala ponoviti.* / *Trebala bi ponoviti.*)
3. **Listening (4):** listen-type ×2 (*Htio bih upisati tečaj hrvatskog.* · *Da imam vremena, došao bih.*), listen + MC ×2 (hear a request → "friend or professor?" ‖ hear a condition → "will it happen or not?")
4. **Reading (3):** a 6-line note from a student to a professor (taught vocabulary only, three conditional frames in it) + 3 MC comprehension hinging on WHO is being asked to do what and whether the condition is real
5. **Writing task (attached to the test, AI-graded, never gates):** see below.
Example item: MC — *___ li mi mogli objasniti raspored?* (Bi / Biste) → Biste (you are speaking to a professor — formal Vi) · listen + MC — TTS: *Kad bih imao novca, upisao bih tečaj.* "Does he have the money?" → no, that's why it's the conditional.

## Writing task (B1 unit shape)

- **Title:** Što bih studirao i zašto (What I would study and why)
- **Task (EN):** Write 80–150 words in Croatian about education. Say what you studied or are studying now, then what you **would** study if you could start again (*Studirao/Studirala bih…*) and what you would need for it (*Da imam…, upisao/upisala bih…*). Give one piece of advice to someone starting the same path (*Na tvom mjestu bih… / Trebalo bi…*), and finish with one real plan in the future (*Ako…, …ću…*). Use the L5 scaffold.
- **Word range:** 80–150 (counter shown)
- **Required elements (the rubric checks all four):** ≥2 conditional sentences with correct participle agreement · one unreal condition (da + present or kad bih) · one advice frame · one real condition in future I
- **Model text (HR, ~95 words, revealable):** authored in the content file — every conditional form correct, only taught vocabulary, all four required elements present and identifiable
- **Rubric focus:** conditional formation and participle agreement (bih + htio/htjela), real-vs-unreal condition choice, clitic position (bih in second position, not sentence-final), register consistency (ti or Vi, not both); task completion (all four required elements present)

## NotesDoc focus per lesson

- **L1:** the full bih/bi/bi/bismo/biste/bi table + the participle-swap flow + the clitic train with the aux slot lit up; mistakes: \*Ja bi studirao ❌ → Ja **bih** studirao ✅ (1sg is the one with -h), \*bih htjela → for a woman it is **htjela bih** but the clitic is still bih — the *participle* changes, not the clitic (\*bila bih htjela is Conditional II, B2), \*Nebih ❌ → **ne bih** ✅ (two words), sentence-initial \*Bih došao ❌ → **Došao bih** ✅ / **Ja bih došao** ✅ (a clitic can never open a clause); hook: "perfekt with the clitic swapped — you learned this in Unit 8 and didn't know it"
- **L2:** the politeness ladder in full + the frame table + the ti/Vi callout; mistakes: \*Bi li Vi mogli…? ❌ → **Biste li mogli…?** ✅ (Vi takes biste), \*Htio bih da mi objasnite ❌ (that is the U21 da-clause) → **Biste li mi mogli objasniti?** ✅, \*Htjela bih upisati se tečaj ❌ → **upisati tečaj** ✅, using the blunt imperative with a stranger; hook: the ladder — "one rung up for every stranger"
- **L3:** the two-roads contrast printable + the kad bih ⇄ da + present equivalence; mistakes: \*Ako bih imao vremena ❌ → **Ako imam** ✅ (real) / **Kad bih imao** ✅ (unreal), \*Da imam vremena, doći ću ❌ (halves crossed) → **Da imam vremena, došao bih** ✅, \*Kad budem imao vremena (that's U22 — correct Croatian, just not yet), forgetting participle agreement in the main clause (\*Ana bi došao ❌); hook: "ako = the fork you can still take · da/kad bih = the road with the barrier"
- **L4:** the four-frame usage map + the trebao bi / trebalo bi callout + the riječ i-declension and razumjeti tables; mistakes: \*Ana trebalo bi ponoviti ❌ → **Ana bi trebala ponoviti** ✅ (personal frame agrees), \*Trebala bi vježbati svaki dan (as a general truth about everyone) → **Trebalo bi** ✅ (impersonal), \*Na tvoj mjesto ❌ → **Na tvom mjestu** ✅ (LOC), \*razumim ❌ → **razumijem** ✅; hook: "four volumes of the same advice — pick the one that fits the person"
- **L5:** the four-jobs bracket (the unit's printable, also the writing study sheet) + the scaffold with the model text; mistakes: writing the composition in English order and translating it (build from the scaffold instead), losing register halfway (starting with ti and finishing with Vi), stacking every sentence on *bih* until the text has no facts in it (the scaffold requires real past/present sentences too), forgetting the one required real condition in future I; the notes page IS the pre-writing checklist
