# B1 — Fluency Foundations (Units 17–24): Lesson Enumeration

Every unit and lesson enumerated. These are fleshed into full §11-template unit specs (like `curriculum/A1/unit-1.md`) as Phase 4 approaches — grammar sequencing here is binding, wording of lessons may be refined then.

B1 unit shape: 5 lessons + 300–500-word adapted story + unit test + **guided writing task (80–150 words, AI rubric-graded)**. From B1 on, teach slides shrink and integration/production slides grow; the AI tutor becomes a weekly guided activity.

## Unit 17 — Mediji (media & news basics) · Aspect mastery I

| L | Lesson | Grammar point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Novine i portali | Aspect pair formation: prefixation (pisati→napisati, raditi→uraditi) — recognizing the pattern |
| 2 | Na televiziji | Pair formation: -nu- perfectives (dizati→dignuti) and stem changes (kupovati→kupiti, davati→dati) |
| 3 | Slušam radio | Suppletive pairs (govoriti/reći, dolaziti/doći); aspect with phase-of-day habits |
| 4 | Vijest dana | Choosing aspect in past narration (background imperfective vs event perfective — story spine) |
| 5 | Moji mediji | Consolidation: aspect-choice drills across past/future; writing task: "Što sam jučer čitao/gledao" |

Vocab: novine, vijest, članak, naslov, portal, emisija, program, voditelj, gledatelj, slušatelj, objaviti/objavljivati, javiti/javljati, prenositi/prenijeti…

## Unit 18 — Obrazovanje (education & learning) · Conditional I

| L | Lesson | Grammar point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | U školi i na fakultetu | Conditional I formation (bih/bi/bismo + l-participle) |
| 2 | Molim Vas… | Polite requests & wishes (Htio bih…, Biste li mogli…, Mogao bih) |
| 3 | Da imam vremena… | Hypotheticals: kad/da + conditional patterns |
| 4 | Učenje jezika | Savjeti (advice): Na tvom mjestu bih…; trebalo bi + infinitive |
| 5 | Moje obrazovanje | Consolidation; writing: "Što bih studirao i zašto" |

Vocab: osnovna/srednja škola, razred, ocjena, znanje, vještina, tečaj, diploma, zadaća, položiti/polagati ispit, upisati, naučiti…

## Unit 19 — Mišljenja (opinions & debate) · Relative clauses: koji through all cases

| L | Lesson | Grammar point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mislim da… | Opinion frames (mislim/smatram/čini mi se da…) + koji in NOM |
| 2 | Čovjek kojeg poznajem | koji in ACC/GEN (animate kojeg vs inanimate koji) |
| 3 | Grad u kojem živim | koji in LOC/DAT with prepositions |
| 4 | Ljudi s kojima radim | koji in INS + plural forms; full koji declension table |
| 5 | Debata | Agreeing/disagreeing register (slažem se, nisam siguran, s jedne strane…); writing: argument paragraph |

Vocab: mišljenje, stav, razlog, argument, prednost, nedostatak, tema, rasprava, složiti se, tvrditi, vjerovati, sumnjati…

## Unit 21 — Tehnologija (technology & internet) · Indirect speech

| L | Lesson | Grammar point |

## Unit 22 — Kultura i povijest (Croatian culture & history) · Future II; kad/ako/čim

| L | Lesson | Grammar point |
|---|---|---|

## Unit 23 — Osjećaji (emotions & relationships) · Full clitic cluster; verbal nouns

| L | Lesson | Grammar point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kako se osjećaš? | Emotion vocab + DAT/ACC experiencer patterns review (drago mi je, strah me je) |
| 2 | Dao sam joj ga | Full clitic-cluster ordering: li–aux–DAT–ACC/GEN–se–je (train diagram, drills) |
| 3 | Je na kraju | je-final rule; se+je drop; cluster in questions (Jesi li mu ga dao?) |
| 4 | Veze i prijateljstva | Verbal nouns (-nje: druženje, putovanje, učenje) + uses |
| 5 | Pismo prijatelju | Consolidation; writing: personal letter (80–150 words) |

Vocab: osjećaj, sreća, tuga, ljutnja, strah, ljubav, veza, brak, prijateljstvo, povjerenje, zaljubiti se, posvađati se, pomiriti se, nedostajati…

## Unit 24 — Planovi (plans, careers, decisions) · Word formation; plural consolidation

| L | Lesson | Grammar point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Male stvari | Diminutives (-ić, -ica, -ce: gradić, kućica) & augmentatives (receptive) |
| 2 | Novi počeci | Verb prefixes as meaning-makers (do-, od-, pre-, iz-, za- : doći/otići/prijeći/izaći/zaći) |
| 3 | Kako se kaže…? | Adjective→adverb (-o/-e), noun→adjective (-ski, -an) derivation |
| 4 | Svi padeži, množina | All-case PLURAL consolidation with adjectives (novi gradovi → novih gradova → novim gradovima…) |
| 5 | Moj plan za godinu | Level capstone; writing: goals essay (120–150 words) |

Vocab: plan, cilj, odluka, prilika, izbor, karijera, uspjeh, neuspjeh, promjena, iskustvo, odlučiti se, planirati, postići, pokušati…

## → B1 Checkpoint Exam (`checkpoint-exam.md`, authored in Phase 4)

4 skills + essay: listening (2 TTS passages), reading (adapted 400-word text), grammar cloze (aspect + koji + clitics weighted), speaking (3 AI-graded prompts incl. a 60-second opinion monologue), essay 100–150 words (AI rubric: task/grammar/vocab/coherence, 1–5 each). ~50 items, pass ≥ 70%.
