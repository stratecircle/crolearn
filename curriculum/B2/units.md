# B2 — Proficiency (Units 25–32): Lesson Enumeration

Every unit and lesson enumerated; fleshed into full §11-template specs as Phase 4 reaches them. Binding for grammar sequencing.

B2 unit shape: 5 lessons + 500–800-word authentic-register text + unit test + **essay (200–300 words, AI rubric-graded)** + **AI tutor debate/role-play session** (scripted scenario per unit). Teach slides are minimal; most slides are analysis, production, and discussion prep. Listening moves to longer TTS passages at natural speed.

|---|---|---|

## → B2 Final Exam (`final-exam.md`, authored in Phase 4)

Modeled loosely on B2 certificate exams: listening (3 passages, natural speed), reading (2 authentic-register texts, ~700 words total), grammar/vocab (aspect, conditionals, passives, subordination weighted), writing (250-word argumentative essay, AI rubric 5 dimensions), speaking (recorded: 90-second monologue + AI role-play + debate turn, AI-graded). ~70 items, ~90 min (can be split across 2 days), pass ≥ 70%. Passing = course complete: **Čestitamo, govorite hrvatski!**
