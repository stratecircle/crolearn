# A1 Unit 7 — Kupovina (Shopping & Money)

## Overview

The GENITIVE unit — the third case, and the great chunk-payoff of A1: **iz Kanade (U2), čaša vode-style café orders, and "Sedam je sati" (U6) all get analyzed here.** Genitive arrives through its most concrete uses: quantities, "there's no…", origin/direction prepositions, and prices. Numbers grow to 1000 so real shopping works. The number+case government pattern (2–4 vs 5+) is taught as a drilled fixed pattern, not full theory.

## Can-do objectives

- I can buy clothes and food, ask prices, and understand answers (Koliko košta? Pet eura.)
- I can express quantities (a glass of water, a kilo of cheese, a lot of time)
- I can say something is missing or unavailable (Nema kruha.)
- I can say where things/people are from and how far things go (iz, od, do)
- I can count to 1000 and use the right noun form after numbers

## New vocabulary (~40 items)

**L1 (quantities):** čaša (f, glass) · boca (f, bottle) · šalica (f, cup) · kilogram (m) · litra (f) · komad (m, piece) · malo (little/few) · puno (a lot) · dosta (enough) · previše (too much)

**L2 (availability & shops):** trgovina (f, shop) · tržnica (f, market) · pekarnica (f, bakery) · kruh (m, bread — recycled U4) · mlijeko (n, recycled) · nema (there isn't, chunk→analyzed) · blizu (near, + GEN) · daleko od (far from, chunk)

**L3 (money & the bracket):** novac (m, money) · cijena (f, price) · euro (m) · cent (m) · račun (m, recycled U4) · koštati (to cost) · platiti (to pay) · jeftin (cheap) · skup (expensive)

**L4 (tens & colors — `closed-set`):** dvadeset · trideset · četrdeset · pedeset · šezdeset · sedamdeset · osamdeset · devedeset · sto · tisuća (f) · crven · plav · zelen · žut · crn · bijel — colors drill naturally on price tags (*Plava majica košta trideset eura.*) and are recycled with agreement in L5

**L5 (clothes):** majica (f, t-shirt) · hlače (f pl, trousers) · cipele (f pl, shoes) · jakna (f, jacket) · haljina (f, dress) · košulja (f, shirt) · veličina (f, size) · nositi (wear) · probati (try on) · kupovati (buy) · tražiti (look for) — colors recycled from L4 with NOM/ACC agreement

## Lessons

### L1 — Čaša vode (GEN singular: quantities)

- **Grammar micro-point:** GEN sg endings: masc/neut **-a**, fem **-e**. Use #1: quantity + GEN (čaša vode, kilogram sira, litra mlijeka, puno posla). → grammar-reference §3
- **Required visuals:** GEN-green declension table (sg, all genders) next to the known NOM/ACC columns — the case table is now 3 columns wide and grows; quantity-shelf diagram (🥛 čaša mlijeka, 🧀 kilogram sira, 💧 boca vode) with endings green-highlighted; GEN usage-map starter card (first box QUANTITY — the full four-box map is completed in L2)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Kavu, molim works. Now order like a local: velika čaša vode." → Teach: GEN sg table + quantity rule → Examples + TTS: čaša vode · boca vina · kilogram kruha · malo vremena (ime-type flagged) → MC ×3 (pick form: čaša ___ — voda/vode/vodu) → fill ×3 (shelf diagram prompts) → type ×2: "a bottle of water", "a lot of time" → match ×1: quantity word ↔ shelf picture → listen-type ×2 (*Molim čašu vode.* · *Kilogram sira, molim.*) → interleave ×2 (U4 ACC orders — contrast Pijem vodu vs čaša vode) → speak: Molim čašu vode. (čašu = ACC, vode = GEN — the whole phrase decoded!) → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U4 café ACC, U6 vrijeme
- **SRS:** vocab + GEN-form cloze cards (čaša ___ (voda) → vode)
- **Watch-out (NotesDoc):** ~~čaša voda~~ ❌; masc/neut both -a (sira, mlijeka) — don't invent -o forms

### L2 — Nema kruha! (nema + GEN; iz/od/do + GEN)

- **Grammar micro-point:** existential negation **nema + GEN** (Nema kruha. Nema Ane.) · core GEN prepositions **iz** (from/out of), **od** (from), **do** (to/until), **blizu** (near). **Payoff: iz Kanade / iz Zagreba (U2 chunks) fully analyzed.** → grammar-reference §3, §14
- **Required visuals:** empty-shelf diagram (🍞❌ Nema kruha); GEN usage-map (mandated for every case): four boxes — QUANTITY · NEMA · FROM/OF (iz/od) · NEAR (blizu/do) — each arrowed to an example; U2 flashback card: "iz Kanade — now you know why -e"
- **Slide arc:** Hook: bakery scene, TTS: »Oprostite, nema kruha!« → Teach: nema + GEN → MC ×3 → Teach: iz/od/do/blizu + GEN with usage-map → Examples + TTS: Tom je iz Kanade. Tržnica je blizu trga. Radim od sedam do tri. → fill ×3 → type ×2: "There's no milk", "I'm from Canada" → reorder: kruha / danas / nema → listen-type ×2 → speak: answer *Odakle si?* with a full sentence (*Ja sam iz Kanade.* — the U2 chunk, now decoded) → interleave ×3 (U2 origins, U5 blizu-locations) → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U2 (odakle si — re-analyzed), U5 city places
- **SRS:** GEN-preposition cloze; nema-pattern cards
- **Watch-out:** ~~Nema kruh~~ ❌; od sedam do tri (times stay in the sati chunk system)
- *Authoring deviation (no-surprise-material): the reorder tile "danas" is untaught (only a one-off U4 story gloss), so the built lesson uses the taught **ovdje** (U5) instead → "Ovdje nema kruha." Also, the od…do time example drops "Radim/otvorena" (untaught) and shows "Idem od kuće do trgovine." + the bare frame "od sedam do tri".*

### L3 — Dva piva, pet kava (Numbers govern cases)

- **Grammar micro-point:** the number government pattern (as drilled pattern, not theory): **1 → NOM sg · 2–4 → GEN sg · 5+ → GEN pl** (GEN pl -a form given as vocabulary for now: eura, kuna, kava, piva, ljudi). **U6's "pet sati" decoded.** → grammar-reference §13
- **Required visuals:** the mandated **bracket diagram**: 1 | 2 3 4 | 5+ with color-coded noun forms (jedan euro | dva eura | pet eura); beer-table meme grid (jedno pivo, dva piva, pet piva)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Order 2 beers and 5 coffees without breaking grammar." → Teach: bracket diagram → Examples + TTS: jedan euro / dva eura / pet eura · jedna kava / dvije kave / pet kava (dva/dvije flagged!) → MC ×4 (number → pick form) → fill ×3 → type ×2: "three beers", "five coffees" → listen-type ×2 (numbers + nouns) → interleave ×2 (U6 times: pet sati now analyzed) → speak: Dva piva, molim. → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U6 time chunks, U4 café items
- **SRS:** bracket-pattern cloze cards (dva ___ (pivo) → piva); dva/dvije pair
- **Watch-out:** dva piva vs dvije kave (gender!); 21 restarts the cycle (dvadeset jedan euro) — preview card only

### L4 — Koliko košta? (Big numbers & prices)

- **Grammar micro-point:** tens to 1000 (compound: dvadeset pet); price frames Koliko košta? / Koliko koštaju? (sg/pl verb!) · Košta pet eura i pedeset centi. → grammar-reference §13
- **Required visuals:** number-builder diagram (pedeset + šest = 56); price-tag cards with € amounts; košta/koštaju verb-agreement switch card (majica košta / hlače koštaju — hlače is plural-only!)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: real Zagreb price tags via TTS → Teach: tens + compounds → listen-type ×3 (dictation: trideset sedam, devedeset devet…) → Teach: price dialogues + košta/koštaju → MC ×2 → fill ×2 (price tags) → type ×2: "It costs 45 euros" (Košta četrdeset pet eura.) · "The trousers cost 99 euros" (Hlače koštaju devedeset devet eura.) → reorder: koštaju / hlače / koliko → interleave ×3 (L3 brackets with prices: dvadeset dva eura) → speak: Koliko košta jakna? → Recap
- **Interleaving:** L3 pattern under bigger numbers; U2 numbers 1–20
- **SRS:** tens; price-frame cloze; listening-priority (numbers are an ear skill — same rule as U2 L4)
- **Watch-out:** koštaju with hlače/cipele (plural-only nouns!); centi after 5+ (GEN pl -i type, given as vocab)
- *Authoring deviation (no-surprise-material): clothes (majica, hlače) are introduced in **L5**, so L4 cannot drill colours/koštaju on them. The built L4 instead uses already-taught nouns — sg **košta** with kava/kruh, pl **koštaju** with jabuke/knjige; colours on vino/kava/jabuka/sir/more (crveno vino, crna kava). The plural-only hlače/cipele + full ACC colour agreement (crnu jaknu) land in L5 exactly as the spec intends.*

### L5 — U trgovini (Shop dialogues & colors — integration)

- **Grammar micro-point:** none new — full shopping script: Molim Vas… · Tražim majicu. · Imate li…? (li-question recycled from U2) · Mogu li probati? (chunk) · colors as NOM-sg adjectives (recycled agreement from U2/U3: crvena majica, plave hlače)
- **Required visuals:** shop-dialogue flow map (greet → ask → try → price → pay → thank); color swatch grid with agreement endings; clothes rack with gender-colored labels
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS full shop dialogue — "you're both roles by the end." → Teach: script + Imate li → MC ×2 → Teach: colors + agreement (crven/crvena/crveno mini-table) → match: color ↔ item with correct ending → fill: dialogue blanks → type ×2: "I'm looking for a black jacket" (Tražim crnu jaknu — ACC agreement!) → listen-type ×2 → integration: buy an outfit for the story characters (guided chain) → speak: Koliko košta plava majica? → unit-review set: 8 quiz slides sampling L1–L4 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** whole unit; U3 adjective agreement; U4 ACC
- **SRS:** clothes + colors; shop-script chunk cards
- **Watch-out:** ~~crven majica~~ ❌ → crvena ✅ ; Tražim crnu jaknu (adjective declines WITH the noun in ACC)

## Story — «Na tržnici» (At the market)

Baka Marija takes Tom to the Dolac market: quantities everywhere (kilogram rajčica — glossed, pola kilograma sira), a stall has no bread (Nema kruha!), Tom pays and counts change, buys a blue t-shirt nearby. Ana laughs at his bargaining. 120–150 words.
Cast: Tom, baka Marija, Ana. Glossed (≤5): rajčica (tomato), svjež (fresh), skupo (expensive, adv.), sitniš (change), štand (stall).
Sample sentences: »Molim kilogram sira i dvije boce vode.« · »Nema kruha, ali pekarnica je blizu trga.«
Comprehension: 4 MC (Što Tom kupuje? Čega nema? Koliko košta sir? …)
