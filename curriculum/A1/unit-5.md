# A1 Unit 5 — U gradu (City & Directions)

## Overview

The second case arrives: **Lokativ**, the "where am I" case, always with a preposition (u/na). Its payoff is doubled by the direction-vs-location contrast with the already-known ACC (Idem u grad / Ja sam u gradu) — the single most useful minimal pair in early Croatian. `ići` gives the course its first motion verb, ordinals handle floors and addresses, and L5 turns it all into street-survival: asking and giving directions. Direction-giving chunks (Idite! Skrenite!) are memorized wholes — imperative grammar waits for A2 Unit 14, exactly like zvati se waited in U1.

## Can-do objectives

- I can say where I am and where things are in a city (Živim u Zagrebu, muzej je na trgu)
- I can say where I'm going and distinguish it from where I am (u grad vs u gradu)
- I can use ići with the right u/na destinations (na posao, na kavu, u kino)
- I can say my address and which floor I live on
- I can ask for directions and understand a simple answer (ravno, lijevo, desno, blizu)

## New vocabulary (~40 items)

**L1 (city places):** trg (m, square) · ulica (f, street) · muzej (m, museum) · crkva (f, church) · pošta (f, post office) · banka (f, bank) · park (m, park) · centar (m, centre) · živjeti (živim, to live)

**L2 (where/where-to):** gdje (where — location) · kamo (where to — direction) · ovdje (here) · tamo (there) · kino (n, cinema) · kolodvor (m, station) · blizu (near) · daleko (far)

**L3 (going places):** ići (idem, to go) · posao (m, job/work) · fakultet (m, university/faculty) · kazalište (n, theatre) · koncert (m, concert) · zračna luka (f, airport) · tramvaj (m, tram) · autobus (m, bus) · stanica (f, stop) · čekati (čekam, to wait)

**L4 (floors & addresses):** ordinals prvi · drugi · treći · četvrti · peti · šesti · sedmi · osmi · deveti · deseti (one pattern set) · kat (m, floor) · prizemlje (n, ground floor) · zgrada (f, building) · stan (m, apartment) · adresa (f, address)

**L5 (directions):** lijevo (left) · desno (right) · ravno (straight) · pa (then) · most (m, bridge) · hotel (m, hotel) · idite (go! — chunk) · skrenite (turn! — chunk)

## Lessons

### L1 — Gdje si? Lokativ (LOC with u/na: location)

- **Grammar micro-point:** LOC = the location case, NEVER without a preposition; u/na + LOC answers *gdje*. Endings: m/n → -u (grad→gradu, kino→kinu), f -a → -i (škola→školi); sibilarization trap banka→banci (k→c). → grammar-reference §3, §14
- **New vocab:** L1 set
- **Required visuals:** **LOC declension table** (m/f/n columns, endings color-coded `purple-600`); **usage-map diagram** ("Where is it?" box → u gradu · na trgu · u školi arrows); ⚠️ sibilarization card (ban**k**a → u ban**c**i)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "One ending and you can say where anything is — u Zagrebu, na trgu, u parku." → Teach: LOC table + usage map (u = inside, na = on/open space — full na-word list comes in L3) → Examples + TTS: **Živim u Zagrebu.** · **Tom je u muzeju.** · **Ana pije kavu na trgu.** → MC ×3 (Marko je u ___: grad/gradu/grada) → Teach: fem -a→-i + banci trap → fill ×3 (Novac je u ___ (banka) → banci) → type ×2: "I live in Zagreb", "The church is in the centre" (Živim u Zagrebu; Crkva je u centru) → listen-type ×2 (Pošta je u ulici.) → speak: answer *Gdje živiš?* aloud (*Živim u Zagrebu.* — own city) → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U1: kava, škola (u školi — first declined use); U2: grad, more (u gradu, na moru); U4: piti + ACC inside a LOC sentence (Ana pije kavu na trgu)
- **SRS:** L1 vocab; cloze cards LOC endings (u Zagreb__ → -u; u škol__ → -i); trap card "u banci (k→c)"

### L2 — U grad ili u gradu? (Direction ACC vs location LOC)

- **Grammar micro-point:** same prepositions u/na, two cases: motion/direction → ACC (kamo? Idem u grad), location → LOC (gdje? Ja sam u gradu). idem/ideš appear as preview chunks (full paradigm in L3). → grammar-reference §14 (the u/na rule pair)
- **New vocab:** L2 set
- **Required visuals:** **direction-vs-location arrow diagram** (mandated §4): person → 🏠 "u kuću / u kino (ACC, `red-600`)" vs person inside 🏠 "u kući / u kinu (LOC, `purple-600`)"; gdje/kamo question-word pair card; two-column contrast table (red ACC column vs purple LOC column, same nouns)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "One letter changes the meaning: u grad = going there, u gradu = already there." → Teach: arrow diagram + gdje↔kamo pairing → Examples + TTS: **Idem u grad. — Ja sam u gradu.** · **Tom ide na kolodvor. — Tom je na kolodvoru.** · **Kamo ideš? — U kino!** → MC ×4 (pick ACC or LOC: Ana je u kin__; Idem u škol__) → Teach: blizu/daleko + ovdje/tamo as location adverbs → fill ×3 (Kamo ideš? Idem u ___ (grad); Gdje si? U ___ (grad)) → reorder: u / idem / kino → listen-type ×2 → speak: answer both Gdje si? and Kamo ideš? → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U2: Odakle si? now contrasted with gdje/kamo (three where-words); U4: Želim + ACC (Želim ići u kino preview); U1: bok/doviđenja closing the mini-dialogues
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; contrast cloze pairs (Idem u grad__ / Živim u grad__); gdje↔kamo discrimination cards

### L3 — Idem na kavu (ići + idiomatic u/na destinations)

- **Grammar micro-point:** ići fully conjugated (idem, ideš, ide, idemo, idete, idu — infinitive doesn't predict it!); the na-word list (events, open spaces, institutions: na posao, na fakultet, na trg, na koncert, na more, na kavu) vs u-words (enclosed places: u školu, u kino, u crkvu, u muzej). → grammar-reference §7 (irregulars), §14
- **New vocab:** L3 set
- **Required visuals:** ići conjugation table (6 persons, endings bolded); **city-map semantic grid** (§4 vocab-set visual): places drawn on a mini-map, each tagged u or na; na-word list card ("if in doubt: buildings u, events/open spaces na")
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Idem na kavu — the most Croatian sentence there is." → Teach: ići table → Examples + TTS: **Idemo na kavu!** · **Marko ide na posao.** · **Ana i Tom idu u kino.** → MC ×3 (Mi ___ u kazalište: idemo/idete/idu) → Teach: u vs na map + na-word list (na posao but u školu — memorize the list, logic only helps) → match: place ↔ u/na column → fill ×3 (Idem ___ koncert; Ana je ___ fakultetu) → type ×2: "We are going to the sea", "I am waiting for the tram" (Idemo na more; Čekam tramvaj) → listen-type ×2 → speak: Kamo ideš danas? → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U2: more (na more/na moru), numbers for tram lines (tramvaj broj pet); U4: htjeti (Hoću ići na koncert); U1: čaj/kava (idemo na kavu, na čaj)
- **SRS:** L3 vocab; cloze all 6 ići forms; na-word list cards (posao→na, kino→u…); chunk "Idem na kavu/na posao/na more"

### L4 — Treći kat (Ordinals 1–10, addresses & floors)

- **Grammar micro-point:** ordinals prvi–deseti behave like adjectives (prvi/prva/prvo); floors use na + LOC with the adjective LOC ending -om/-em (na prvom katu, na trećem katu — soft stem → -em); addresses stay simple: name + number (Ilica 15), lived-in as u + LOC (u Ilici). → grammar-reference §13 (ordinals), §4 (adjective LOC row)
- **New vocab:** L4 set
- **Required visuals:** **elevator/floor diagram** (building cross-section: prizemlje → prvi kat → … → deseti kat, each labeled with na + LOC form); ordinal grid 1.–10. with the -i adjective ending highlighted; address card anatomy (street + number + kat + stan)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Which floor? Third. In Croatian you now can say it — and live there: na trećem katu." → Teach: ordinal grid (prvi, drugi, treći… — treći is the odd one) → MC ×3 (5. = peti/petak/pet) → Teach: floors: na + ordinal-om + katu; prizemlje → u prizemlju → Examples + TTS: **Živim na trećem katu.** · **Stan broj sedam je na drugom katu.** · **Moja adresa je Ilica petnaest.** → fill ×3 (Banka je u ___ (prizemlje) → prizemlju; Živim na ___ katu (2.) → drugom) → type ×2: "I live on the fourth floor" (Živim na četvrtom katu) → listen-type ×2 (address dictation: Ulica broj devet) → reorder: katu / na / živim / prvom → speak: say which floor you live on (*Živim na drugom katu.*) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U2: numbers 1–20 (address numbers, broj), koliko; U3: moj/tvoj (moja adresa, tvoj stan); U5 L1: zgrada/centar in LOC
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; ordinal cards (3. → treći); cloze "na ___ katu (1.) → prvom"; chunk "Moja adresa je…"

- **SRS:** L5 vocab; dialogue-chunk cloze (Idite ___, pa skrenite ___); route-following listening cards

## Story — «Tom traži muzej» (Tom looks for the museum)

Tom wants to see a museum. Ana explains: the museum is in the centre, near the cathedral — he can go by tram. Tom waits at the stop, rides the tram and watches the city. On the square he asks a gospođa for directions; she sends him straight ahead and right. He finds the museum — but at the entrance a sign says it is closed, so Tom goes for a coffee on the square instead. 100–130 words, present tense, NOM + ACC + LOC only.
Cast: Tom, Ana (+ anonymous gospođa). New glossed words (≤5): karta (ticket), ulaz (entrance), katedrala (cathedral), zatvoreno (closed).
Sample sentences: »Oprostite, gospođo, gdje je muzej?« — »Idite ravno, pa skrenite desno. Muzej je na trgu, blizu.« · »Tom je u tramvaju i gleda grad.«
Comprehension: 5 MC questions (Kamo Tom ide? Gdje je muzej? Je li muzej daleko? Što Tom čeka? Kamo Tom ide na kraju?).
