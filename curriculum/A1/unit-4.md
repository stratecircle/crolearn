# A1 Unit 4 — Hrana i piće (Food & Café)

## Overview

The first real case. ACC singular lands here because Units 1–3 planted its forms as chunks (»Kavu, molim«, »Imam sestru«) — L1 is the payoff lesson where those chunks get X-rayed. The unit also builds the whole present-tense system (-am, -im, -em classes) on food verbs, then the animate-ACC rule and htjeti, and cashes everything in at the café — the course's first complete transactional scenario. ACC = red everywhere.

## Can-do objectives

- I can name common foods, drinks, and meals
- I can use direct objects correctly (Pijem kavu, jedem kruh, čekam konobara)
- I can conjugate verbs of all three present-tense classes (gledam, volim, pijem)
- I can say what I like, want, and don't want (Volim ribu, neću pivo)
- I can order food and drink in a café and ask for the bill

## New vocabulary (~40 items)

**L1 (foods — ACC material):** kruh (m, bread) · sir (m, cheese) · jabuka (f, apple) · juha (f, soup) · salata (f, salad) · riba (f, fish) · meso (n, meat) · mlijeko (n, milk) · sok (m, juice)

**L2 (-am verbs + meals):** gledati (gledam, to watch/look at) · kuhati (kuham, to cook) · čekati (čekam, to wait for) · ručati (ručam, to have lunch) · večerati (večeram, to have dinner) · doručak (m, breakfast) · ručak (m, lunch) · večera (f, dinner)

**L3 (-im/-em verbs + drinks):** piti (pijem, to drink) · jesti (jedem, to eat — irregular) · voljeti (volim, to love/like) · željeti (želim, to want/wish) · pivo (n, beer) · vino (n, wine) · voće (n, fruit) · povrće (n, vegetables)

**L4 (htjeti + states + treats):** htjeti (hoću, to want) · gladan (hungry) · žedan (thirsty) · kolač (m, cake) · sladoled (m, ice cream) · čokolada (f, chocolate)

**L5 (café):** jelovnik (m, menu) · račun (m, bill) · kafić (m, café) · restoran (m, restaurant) · još (more/another) · Dobar tek! (enjoy your meal) · Živjeli! (cheers)

## Lessons

### L1 — Akuzativ! (ACC singular: fem -u; masc/neut inanimate = NOM)

- **Grammar micro-point:** the direct-object case: fem -a → **-u**; masc/neut inanimate = same as NOM (no change!); animate masc deferred to L4. This is why U1's »Kavu, molim« and U3's »Imam sestru« look the way they do. → grammar-reference §3 (ACC rows)
- **New vocab:** L1 set
- **Required visuals:** ACC declension table (NOM → ACC per gender, endings color-coded — ACC `red-600`); **usage-map diagram** (when do I use ACC? → boxes: "thing you have (imam)", "thing you ask for (molim)", "later: thing verbs act on" with arrows to example phrases); chunk-X-ray card (Kavu, molim / Imam sestru with the -u highlighted red)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Since Unit 1 you've said Kavu, molim. Today you find out WHY it's kavu." → Teach: ACC table + usage map (only fem changes; m/n inanimate stays = NOM) → Examples + TTS, endings red-highlighted: **Imam jabuku. Juhu, molim! Imam kruh i sir.** → MC ×3 (Molim ___: salata/salatu/salate) → Teach: the "no change" half — sok, meso, mlijeko in ACC look identical (that's the rule, not an exception) → fill ×3 (Imam ___. (riba → ribu)) → type ×2: "I have an apple", "Soup, please" (Imam jabuku; Juhu, molim) → listen-type ×2 (Imam salatu i sok.) → speak: ask for two items aloud (*Juhu i kruh, molim!*) → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U1: Kavu, molim / Izvolite / Hvala replayed and X-rayed; U3: Imam sestru / Imam mačku chunks re-graded as regular ACC
- **SRS:** L1 vocab; ACC cloze cards (kava→kavu, juha→juhu, riba→ribu + "sok→sok, no change"); retired-chunk cards upgraded to analyzed grammar cards

### L2 — Gledam, kuham, čekam (-am class present tense)

- **Grammar micro-point:** the -am class (gledati type): -am -aš -a -amo -ate -aju; negation of ordinary verbs = **ne + verb**, two words, never fused (ne kuham — unlike nisam/nemam). → grammar-reference §7
- **New vocab:** L2 set
- **Required visuals:** gledati conjugation table (6 persons, endings bolded and colored); "three fused, everything else free" negation card (nisam/nemam fused ↔ ne gledam two words); meals timeline strip (doručak ☀️ → ručak 🌤️ → večera 🌆)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "One ending set, hundreds of verbs — the -am class is the workhorse." → Teach: gledati table (the -m -š -∅ -mo -te rhythm from imati/govoriti, now the full official pattern) → Examples + TTS: **Kuham juhu. Čekamo Anu. Tom večera ribu.** → MC ×3 (Mi ___ ručak: kuhamo/kuhate/kuhaju) → Teach: ne + verb negation + meals vocab → fill ×3 (Ana ne ___ meso. (kuha)) → type ×2: "I am waiting for Ana", "We are cooking lunch" (Čekam Anu; Kuhamo ručak) → reorder: juhu / baka / kuha → listen-type ×2 → speak: say what you are doing (*Kuham juhu i čekam Anu.*) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U3: family as subjects (Baka kuha juhu; Moja mama večera), imati forms alongside the new class; U2: numbers (Večera je u… deferred — used as counting drill answers)
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; cloze all 6 gledati endings; negation card (ne + verb vs nisam/nemam)

### L3 — Volim, pijem, jedem (-im/-em classes + ACC practice)

- **Grammar micro-point:** the other two classes: -im (voljeti: volim…vole) and -em (piti: pijem…piju); jesti is irregular (jedem…jedu); the infinitive doesn't predict the class — learn 1sg with every verb. → grammar-reference §7
- **New vocab:** L3 set
- **Required visuals:** three-class master conjugation table (gledam | volim | pijem side by side, 6 persons, endings bolded and colored; 3pl row highlighted: gledaju / **vole** / **piju**); "learn the 1sg!" flag card; food-plate semantic grid (voće/povrće/meso/riba)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Two more ending sets and the whole present tense is yours." → Teach: master table, 3pl trap highlighted → Examples + TTS: **Pijem kavu, a Ana pije čaj. Volim voće, ali ne volim povrće. Jedeš li meso? — Ne, jedem ribu.** → MC ×3 (Oni ___ pivo: piju/pijemo/pije) → Teach: jesti → jedem irregular + željeti (želim — polite wanting, the café verb of L5) → fill ×3 (Djeca ___ mlijeko. (piju) · Ja ___ salatu. (jedem)) → type ×2: "They love wine", "I don't drink beer" (Vole vino; Ne pijem pivo) → listen-type ×2 (Volim juhu i kruh.) → speak: say one thing you love and one you don't (Volim… Ne volim…) → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U2: govorim slotted into the -im column (it was the preview!); U1: čaj, kava, voda as ACC objects (Pijem vodu); U3: djeca as 3pl subject (Djeca jedu)
- **SRS:** L3 vocab (each verb card carries its 1sg!); cloze 3pl traps (oni vole / piju / jedu / gledaju); ACC + verb combo cloze (Pijem ___ (kava))

### L4 — Čekam konobara (masc animate ACC + htjeti)

- **Grammar micro-point:** animate masculine nouns (people, animals) take **-a** in ACC sg (konobar → konobara, Marko → Marka) — inanimate stays = NOM; the same -a form returns as GEN in Unit 7. Plus htjeti: hoću…hoće, negation **neću** (the third fused negation). → grammar-reference §3 (ACC anim = GEN), §6 (htjeti)
- **New vocab:** L4 set
- **Required visuals:** animate/inanimate decision diagram (arrow fork: "alive? 🧍🐕 → -a · thing? 🍞 → no change", endings in ACC red); htjeti conjugation table (affirmative + neću column); fused-negation collection card (nisam · nemam · neću — the complete set)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "One last piece of ACC: people. Čekam konobara — not konobar." → Teach: animate rule + decision diagram → Examples + TTS: **Čekam konobara. Ana gleda Marka. Tom čeka prijatelja.** → MC ×3 (Gledam ___: brat/brata/bratu) → Teach: htjeti table + neću; register note: hoću is direct, želim is politer (full café use in L5) → fill ×3 (___ li kolač? (Hoćeš) · Ne, ___ kolač, hoću sladoled. (neću)) → type ×2: "I am waiting for my brother", "I want ice cream" (Čekam brata; Hoću sladoled) → listen-type ×2 (Hoćeš li sok ili vodu?) → speak: answer Jesi li gladan/gladna? Jesi li žedan/žedna? → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U3: Imam brata / Imam psa chunks now fully explained (animate -a!); U2: professions as ACC objects (Čekamo liječnika); U1: sretan-pattern fleeting a echoed in gladan/gladna, žedan/žedna
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; cloze all htjeti forms + neću; animate-ACC cloze (čekam + konobar → konobara, prijatelj → prijatelja, pas → psa); fused-negation set card

### L5 — U kafiću (Ordering: Želim/Molim + ACC, unit review)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new machinery — the café transaction script: Želim/Molim + ACC, jedan/jednu agreeing in ACC (Jednu kavu i jedan sok, molim), Račun, molim. (Title phrase »u kafiću« used as a set phrase — location case arrives in Unit 5.) → grammar-reference §3, §7
- **New vocab:** L5 set
- **Required visuals:** café dialogue flow diagram (greet → order (Želim… molim) → Izvolite → Dobar tek → Račun, molim → Hvala/Doviđenja); jelovnik mock-up card (menu items with genders color-dotted); ordering-frame card (Želim + [red ACC slot], molim)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS plays a full café exchange — "your first complete real-world win." → Teach: dialogue flow + ordering frames → Examples + TTS: **Želim jednu kavu i jedan sok, molim. Račun, molim! Dobar tek! — Hvala!** → MC ×3 (polite order: Hoću kavu / Želim kavu, molim / Kava!) → fill ×3: cloze the dialogue (Konobar: ___! Gost: Hvala! (Izvolite)) → reorder: molim / juhu / jednu / želim → type ×2: "One beer, please", "The bill, please" (Jedno pivo, molim; Račun, molim) → listen-type ×2 (Želite li još nešto? — nešto glossed on-slide) → speak: order a full meal aloud from the mock jelovnik → integration ×2: Marko serves Tom and Ana (story cast) → Recap + unit-review slide set (8 quiz slides sampling L1–L4)
- **Interleaving:** U1: Izvolite / Hvala / Doviđenja courtesy loop; U2: numbers in orders (jedan, dva as counting), Koliko je to?; U3: possessives (Moj račun, molim!)
- **SRS:** L5 vocab; café-script chunk cards (order↔response cloze pairs); jedan/jednu/jedno agreement cards

## Story — «Dobar tek!» (Enjoy your meal!)

Tom and Ana are at Marko's café, and today Tom orders in Croatian for the first time. Ana wants soup and bread; Tom is very hungry and wants fish and salad, but no beer — he drinks water. Marko brings everything and wishes them Dobar tek. Tom asks for the bill in Croatian, and Marko says his Croatian is good now — Tom is a happy guest. 100–140 words, present tense, ACC sg throughout.
Cast: Tom, Ana, Marko. New glossed words (≤5): danas (today), gost (guest), onda (then), nešto (something).
Sample sentences: »Ja želim juhu i kruh. A ti?« — »Ja sam jako gladan — želim ribu i salatu.« · »Hoćeš li pivo?« — »Ne, hvala. Pijem vodu.«
Comprehension: 4 MC questions (Što želi Ana? Je li Tom gladan? Što Tom pije? Tko je konobar?).

## Unit test blueprint (17–19 items, pass ≥ 70%)

1. **Vocab (5):** match food HR↔EN ×2, MC ×2 (povrće = ?), meal-order MC ×1 (doručak/ručak/večera sequencing)
2. **Grammar (7):** ACC fill ×2 (Pijem ___. (kava → kavu) · Jedem ___. (meso — no change)), animate ACC fill ×1 (Čekam ___. (konobar → konobara)), verb-class fill ×2 (Oni ___ vino. (piju) · Mi ___ juhu. (kuhamo)), htjeti/neću fill ×1 (Ja ___ sladoled, hoću kolač. (neću)), ne + verb MC ×1
3. **Listening (4):** listen-type ×2 (Želim jednu kavu, molim. / Volim ribu.), listen + MC meaning ×2 (Ne jedem meso.)
4. **Reading (3):** café micro-dialogue (order → Izvolite → račun) + 3 MC comprehension
Example item: fill — »Ana ___ Marka.« (čeka/gleda per picture) · type — order "one soup and one juice" politely (Jednu juhu i jedan sok, molim).
