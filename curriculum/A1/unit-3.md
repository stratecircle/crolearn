# A1 Unit 3 — Moja obitelj (Family & People)

## Overview

The last all-nominative unit: possessives and plurals squeeze maximum mileage out of NOM before the first real case (ACC) lands in Unit 4. Possessive agreement recycles the U2 gender/adjective machinery on new words; NOM plural completes the noun's "identity card" (gender + sg + pl). `imati` arrives as a fully conjugated verb but its objects stay **fixed-phrase chunks** ("Imam brata i sestru" — *chunk, not yet analyzed*), deliberately planting forms that Unit 4 L4 will X-ray.

## Can-do objectives

- I can name my family members and say whose they are (moja majka, njegov brat)
- I can ask and answer "whose is this?" (Čiji je to pas?)
- I can talk about more than one person or thing (studenti, sestre, sela)
- I can say what family members and pets I have and don't have (Imam sestru, nemam psa)
- I can describe people simply (Moj brat je visok i mlad)

## New vocabulary (~40 items)

**L1 (core family + my/your/our):** obitelj (f, family — consonant-final f!) · majka / mama (f, mother/mom) · otac / tata (m, father/dad — tata is m despite -a!) · brat (m, brother) · sestra (f, sister) · sin (m, son) · kći (f, daughter — irregular) · roditelji (m pl, parents) · moj / tvoj / naš / vaš (my/your/our/your-pl)

**L2 (extended family + his/her/their):** baka (f, grandma) · djed (m, grandpa) · muž (m, husband) · žena (f, wife — U1 word, new sense) · dijete (n, child) · unuk (m) / unuka (f) (grandson/granddaughter) · njegov (his) · njezin (her) · njihov (their) · čiji (whose)

**L3 (groups + size/age adjectives):** djeca (children — collective) · braća (brothers — collective) · ljudi (m pl, people) · velik (big) · malen / mali (small) · mlad (young) · star (old)

**L4 (imati + pets):** imati (imam, to have) · pas (m, dog) · mačka (f, cat) · beba (f, baby) · ptica (f, bird) · kuća (f, house) · auto (m, car — m despite -o!)

**L5 (describing people):** lijep (beautiful/nice) · visok (tall) · nizak (short/low) · osoba (f, person) · pametan (smart — fleeting a: pametna) · jak (strong)

## Lessons

### L1 — Moja obitelj (Possessives moj/tvoj/naš + agreement)

- **Grammar micro-point:** moj/tvoj/naš/vaš agree with the possessed noun like adjectives: moj brat, moja sestra, moje dijete (NOM sg only). → grammar-reference §4 pattern (NOM row); §2 for the tata/obitelj gender traps
- **New vocab:** L1 set
- **Required visuals:** family-tree semantic grid (icons per member, gender-colored); possessive agreement table (moj/moja/moje × tvoj/tvoja/tvoje × naš/naša/naše, endings bolded); trap card: tata is m (naš tata ✅, ne ~~naša~~), obitelj is f (moja obitelj)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Three little words — moj, moja, moje — and the whole family is yours." → Teach: agreement rule + table (same -∅/-a/-o/-e logic as dobar/dobra/dobro from U2!) → Examples + TTS: **Moja majka je učiteljica. Moj otac je inženjer. Naše dijete je malo.** *(malo previewed, taught fully in L3)* → MC ×3 (___ sestra je studentica: moj/moja/moje) → Teach: family tree vocab + traps (tata m, kći f, obitelj f) → match: family icons ↔ HR words → fill ×3 (word bank: Tvoj/Tvoja ___ je liječnica.) → type ×2: "my brother", "our family" (moj brat; naša obitelj) → listen-type ×2 (*Moja majka je učiteljica.* · *Naš tata je inženjer.*) → speak: name two family members with moj/moja → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U2: professions in the examples (učiteljica, inženjer, liječnica — every possessive sentence reuses one); U1: prijatelj/prijateljica (moj prijatelj)
- **SRS:** L1 vocab; cloze cards moj/moja/moje + naš/naša/naše full sets; trap cards (tata = m; obitelj = f)

### L2 — Njegov, njezin, njihov + čiji (his/her/their + whose)

- **Grammar micro-point:** the OWNER picks the word (on → njegov, ona → njezin, oni → njihov), the possessed THING picks the ending (njegova sestra); question word čiji/čija/čije. → grammar-reference §4 pattern; §15 (čiji)
- **New vocab:** L2 set
- **Required visuals:** two-step owner→thing decision diagram (step 1: whose is it? 👨→njegov / 👩→njezin / 👥→njihov; step 2: what gender is the THING? → ending); čiji question cards (čiji 👨‍🦱? čija 👩? čije 🧒?)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "His and her — the trap every English speaker falls into. Dodge it today." → Teach: two-step diagram (owner picks word, thing picks ending) → Examples + TTS: **To je njegova sestra. Njezin muž je konobar. Čije je to dijete? — Njihovo.** → MC ×3 (Ana ima brata → To je ___ brat: njegov/njezin/njihov) → Teach: čiji/čija/čije + extended-family vocab → match: čiji/čija/čije ↔ djed/baka/dijete *(reallocation: originally pas/mačka/dijete/baka, but pas & mačka are L4 vocab — §2b rule 2, no surprise material)* → fill ×3 (Čija je to baka? To je ___ baka. (njezina)) → type ×2: "her grandpa", "their child" (njezin djed; njihovo dijete) → listen-type ×2 (Njegova žena je Njemica.) → speak: answer *Čiji je to pas?* aloud (To je njezin pas.) → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U2: nationalities (Njegova žena je Njemica; Njezin muž je Hrvat); U1: Tko je to? vs new Čiji je to?
- **SRS:** L2 vocab; cloze cards njegov/njezin/njihov agreement sets; contrast card "his sister = njegovA sestra (ending follows sestra, not him!)"

### L3 — Braća i sestre (NOM plural -i/-e/-a)

- **Grammar micro-point:** NOM plural: m → -i (studenti), f → -e (sestre), n → -a (sela); possessives/adjectives take the same endings (moji, moje, moja; stari, stare, stara); irregular trio braća/djeca/ljudi. → grammar-reference §3 (NOM rows), §4 (NOM pl row)
- **New vocab:** L3 set
- **Required visuals:** NOM plural endings table (m/f/n, endings bolded in NOM blue); singular→plural before→after transformation (student → student**i**, sestra → sestr**e**, selo → sel**a**); irregular-trio warning card (brat→braća, dijete→djeca, čovjek→ljudi — with note: braća/djeca take -a agreement: Djeca su mala)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "One brother is brat. Two are… not brati. Meet the plural." → Teach: -i/-e/-a table + transformation visual → Examples + TTS: **Mi smo studenti. Moje sestre su mlade. Njegovi roditelji su stari.** → MC ×3 (pick the plural: žena → žene/ženi/žena) → Teach: irregular trio braća/djeca/ljudi + long plural note (sin → sinovi) → fill ×3 (Naša ___ su mala. (djeca)) → type ×2: "The people are good", "My brothers are tall — Moja braća su visoka" *(visok previewed, taught in L5)* → reorder: su / sestre / moje / mlade → listen-type ×2 (*Moje sestre su mlade.* · *Ljudi su dobri.*) → speak: one plural sentence about your family (*Mi smo velika obitelj.*) → interleave ×3 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U1: student/studentica → studenti/studentice; U2: dobar in the plural (dobri ljudi), Hrvati/Hrvatice from Hrvat/Hrvatica
- **SRS:** L3 vocab; cloze plural cards (brat→braća, dijete→djeca, čovjek→ljudi, sin→sinovi, sestra→sestre); agreement cloze (Djeca su ___ (mala))

### L4 — Imam brata i sestru (imati + fixed-phrase objects)

- **Grammar micro-point:** imati fully conjugated (imam…imaju; negation **nemam**, fused like nisam); its objects are **memorized chunks — chunk, not yet analyzed** (Imam brat**a** / sestr**u** / ps**a** / mačk**u** / kćer — the why is Unit 4's ACC). → grammar-reference §6 (imati/nemam), §7 (-am class preview)
- **New vocab:** L4 set
- **Required visuals:** imati conjugation table (6 persons, endings bolded) + nemam column; ⚠️ chunk card: "Say Imam brata, Imam sestru — memorize the shape, Unit 4 explains it"; pet icons
- **Slide arc:** Hook: "Talk about who and what you HAVE — your second real verb." → Teach: imati table (endings -m -š -∅ -mo -te -ju, rhythm from govoriti!) + nemam → Examples + TTS: **Imam brata i sestru. Imaš li psa? — Nemam psa, imam mačku. Oni imaju bebu.** → MC ×3 (Mi ___ mačku: imamo/imate/imaju) → Teach: the chunk list (brata, sestru, sina, kćer, psa, mačku, bebu, pticu, kuću, auto) + ⚠️ card → fill ×3 (Ana ___ brata. (ima)) → type ×2: "I have a sister", "We don't have a dog" (Imam sestru; Nemamo psa) → listen-type ×2 (Imaš li brata ili sestru?) → speak: answer Imaš li brata ili sestru? truthfully → interleave ×2 → Recap
- **Interleaving:** U2: ili (brata ili sestru), numbers as counting answers (Koliko? — Dva!); U1: da/ne + hvala in mini-dialogues
- **SRS:** L4 vocab; cloze all 6 imati forms + nemam; chunk cards (Imam brata / sestru / psa / mačku / kćer — flagged "why? → Unit 4")

### L5 — Kakva je tvoja obitelj? (Describing people, unit review)

- **Grammar micro-point:** no new machinery — integration: possessives + adjectives (sg from U2, pl from L3) + biti + imati chunks describing real people. → grammar-reference §4
- **New vocab:** L5 set
- **Required visuals:** person-description canvas (figure with labeled adjective pairs: visok↔nizak, mlad↔star, velik↔malen); family-photo description frame (To je… / On je… / Ona ima…)
- **Slide arc:** Hook: TTS plays a full family description — "this is you, in 20 slides." → Teach: opposite pairs + osoba → Examples + TTS: **Moj brat je visok, a ja sam nizak. Baka Marija je stara i jako dobra. Tvoja sestra je lijepa osoba.** → MC ×3 (opposites: visok ↔ ?) → fill ×3 (describe the story cast: Marko je ___ konobar.) → type ×2: "My grandma is old", "Our children are small" (Moja baka je stara; Naša djeca su mala) → reorder: je / osoba / lijepa / ona → listen-type ×2 → speak: describe two family members (2 sentences each) → integration ×2 with story cast → Recap + unit-review sweep of 8 review quiz **interactions** sampling L1–L4 *(counted per §2's interactions measure; shipped as 2 quiz-sets + fill + mc)*
- **Interleaving:** U1: sretan/umoran in descriptions (Moja mama je umorna); U2: dobar/loš/nov + professions (Njegov tata je dobar kuhar)
- **SRS:** L5 vocab; opposite-pair cards (visok↔nizak, mlad↔star, velik↔malen); description-frame cloze (Moja baka je ___ .)

## Story — «Velika obitelj» (A big family)

In the café, Ana shows Tom a photo of her family: her parents, her brother, and baka Marija, who is from Slavonija. Tom asks who everyone is and learns that Ana's brother is tall and young, and that baka Marija has a cat. Tom says he has a sister — she is also Canadian — but no dog, no cat. Ana laughs: their family photo is big, and his is small. 90–130 words, present tense, NOM + possessives + imati-chunks.
Cast: Tom, Ana, baka Marija (in the photo). New glossed words (≤5): fotografija (photo), sladak/slatka (cute), zajedno (together), samo (only).
Sample sentences: »Tko je to?« — »To je moja baka Marija. Ona je stara, ali jako dobra.« · »Imaš li brata ili sestru?« — »Imam sestru. I ona je Kanađanka.«
Comprehension: 4 MC questions (Čija je baka Marija? Odakle je baka Marija? Ima li Tom psa? Kakav je Anin brat? — asked as: Je li Anin brat visok?).

## Unit test blueprint (16–18 items, pass ≥ 70%)

1. **Vocab (5):** match family HR↔EN ×2, MC ×2 (kći = ?), opposite-pair MC ×1 (visok ↔ ?)
2. **Grammar (6):** possessive agreement fill ×2 (___ obitelj je velika. (Naša)), njegov/njezin MC ×1 (Ana ima psa. To je ___ pas.), NOM plural fill ×2 (dijete → ___ su mala. (djeca)), imati/nemam fill ×1 (Oni ___ mačku. (imaju))
3. **Listening (4):** listen-type ×2 (Moja sestra je mlada. / Nemam brata.), listen + MC meaning ×2
4. **Reading (3):** 4-line family-photo dialogue + 3 MC comprehension
Example item: fill — »Čija je to mačka? To je ___ mačka.« (njezina, picture shows Ana) · type — translate "Our parents are old" (Naši roditelji su stari).

## NotesDoc focus per lesson

- **L1:** full moj/tvoj/naš/vaš NOM sg table (m/f/n); mistakes: ~~moj sestra~~ ❌ → moja sestra ✅ (agreement with the THING), ~~moja tata~~ ❌ → moj tata ✅ (tata is m!), ~~moj obitelj~~ ❌ → moja obitelj ✅ (obitelj is f); hook: possessives are just adjectives in disguise (dobar/dobra/dobro = moj/moja/moje)
- **L2:** the his/her decision rule in one line: "owner picks the WORD, thing picks the ENDING"; mistakes: njegov/njezin swapped by matching the noun's gender instead of the owner (Ana's brother = ~~njegov brat~~ ❌ → njezin brat ✅); čiji agreement (~~čiji dijete~~ ❌ → čije dijete ✅)
- **L3:** complete NOM plural table + long-plural note (sin→sinovi, grad→gradovi) + the irregular trio with agreement (braća/djeca take -a: Djeca su dobra, verb plural); mistakes: ~~brati~~ ❌ → braća ✅, ~~čovjeci~~ ❌ → ljudi ✅, ~~djece su~~ ❌ → djeca su ✅ (djeca is the NOM form)
- **L4:** full imati table + nemam (fused negation family: nisam, nemam); the chunk list verbatim with the explicit promise "Unit 4 L1 explains -u, L4 explains brata"; mistakes: ~~Imam brat~~ ❌ → Imam brata ✅ (chunk!), ~~ne imam~~ ❌ → nemam ✅
- **L5:** description sentence frames (To je… / On je… / Ona je… / Imam…); mistakes: plural agreement slips (~~Moji sestre~~ ❌ → Moje sestre ✅), adjective gender defaulting in descriptions (Baka je ~~star~~ ❌ → stara ✅)
