# CroLearn Master Roadmap — Croatian 0 → B2+

This is the single source of truth for the course structure. Every unit spec, lesson, story, test, and notes doc must conform to this map. Companion docs: [01-teaching-method.md](01-teaching-method.md) (how lessons teach), [grammar-reference.md](grammar-reference.md) (ground-truth forms).

## Course at a glance

| | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Units | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| Lessons | 40 | 40 | 40 | 40 |
| Stories | 8 (60–150 words) | 8 (150–300 words) | 8 (300–500 words) | 8 (500–800 words, authentic register) |

**Totals: 32 units · 160 lessons · 32 stories · 36 tests · ~4,700 vocab items · ~14 months to B2.**

Rhythm: **1 unit = 5 lessons + 1 story + 1 unit test** (≈ 8–9 study days including review-heavy days). One lesson ≈ 15–20 min + 10–15 min FSRS review daily.

## The grammar spine (progressive overload)

Croatian's difficulty is concentrated in 7 cases, verb aspect, and clitics. The spine introduces **one heavy thing at a time** and recycles each for at least 2 units before the next:

```
A1: alphabet/phonetics → biti → gender → NOM → present tense → ACC → LOC → GEN(sg) → perfekt
A2: future I → DAT → modals → INS → GEN(pl) → comparatives → VOC → imperative → clitic pronouns → aspect (intro)
B1: aspect (mastery) → conditional I → koji (all cases) → se-passive → indirect speech → future II → clitic cluster (full) → word formation
B2: nuanced aspect → conditional II → passives/participles → verbal adverbs → aorist/imperfect (receptive) → complex subordination → register → idioms
```

Case introduction order (fixed, never violated): **NOM → ACC → LOC → GEN → DAT → INS → VOC**. Case colors (used everywhere — slides, notes, diagrams): NOM `blue` · ACC `red` · GEN `green` · DAT `orange` · LOC `purple` · INS `teal` · VOC `pink`.

## A1 — Foundations (Units 1–8)

Goal: survive and connect in Croatian. Can-do at exit: introduce yourself and family, order food, navigate a city, shop, describe your day and yesterday, in the present and past tense.

| # | Unit (theme) | Vocab domains | Lesson-by-lesson grammar allocation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dobar dan!** — greetings & introductions | greetings, courtesy phrases, yes/no, names, "how are you" | L1 alphabet & phonetics I (vowels, "read as written") · L2 phonetics II (č ć dž đ lj nj š ž, listen-type drills) · L3 `biti` (sam/si/je…) + personal pronouns + pro-drop · L4 basic questions (Kako se zoveš? Tko je to?) · L5 courtesy + review (formal vs informal ti/Vi preview) |
| 2 | **Ja sam iz…** — origins & professions | countries, nationalities, languages, professions | L1 noun gender (-∅/-a/-o,-e) · L2 `biti` negation (nisam) + yes/no questions (jesi li) · L3 adjective agreement NOM sg · L4 numbers 1–20 · L5 govoriti + languages, review |
| 3 | **Moja obitelj** — family & people | family members, people words, basic adjectives (velik, mlad, star…) | L1 possessives moj/tvoj/naš (agreement) · L2 njegov/njezin/njihov + čiji · L3 NOM plural (-i/-e/-a) · L4 `imati` + fixed-phrase objects · L5 describing people, review |
| 4 | **Hrana i piće** — food & café | food, drinks, café/restaurant phrases, meals | L1 **ACC singular** (fem -u; masc/neut inanimate = NOM) · L2 present tense class -am (gledati type) · L3 classes -im/-em (voljeti, piti) + ACC practice · L4 masc animate ACC = GEN form + htjeti · L5 at the café (Želim/Molim + ACC), review |
| 5 | **U gradu** — city & directions | city places, buildings, transport, direction words | L1 **LOC** with u/na (location: U Zagrebu) · L2 direction (u/na + ACC) vs location (LOC) contrast · L3 ići + idiomatic na/u destinations · L4 ordinals 1–10 + addresses/floors · L5 asking/giving directions, review |
| 6 | **Moj dan** — daily routine & time | routine verbs, days, months, times of day | L1 reflexive verbs (zvati se, buditi se, tuširati se) · L2 telling time (full + half hours) · L3 ACC plural · L4 adverbs of frequency + word order · L5 my typical day, review |
| 7 | **Kupovina** — shopping & money | clothes, colors, sizes, money, shops | L1 **GEN singular** (quantities: čaša vode, kilogram sira) · L2 nema + GEN, od/iz/do + GEN intro · L3 numbers 2–4 + GEN sg pattern; 5+ + GEN pl (as fixed pattern) · L4 numbers 20–1000 + prices · L5 in the shop (Koliko košta?), review |
| 8 | **Jučer i danas** — weekend, weather, past | weekend activities, weather, time expressions (jučer, prošli…) | L1 **perfekt** of biti + l-participle formation · L2 perfekt of regular verbs + gender/number agreement · L3 clitic `je` word order (second position, descriptively) · L4 weather + past narration · L5 my weekend, review |

**→ A1 Checkpoint Exam** (`curriculum/A1/checkpoint-exam.md`): 4 sections — listening (TTS), reading (short story-level passage), grammar/vocab cloze, speaking prompts (AI-graded). Pass ≥ 70%.

## A2 — Independence (Units 9–16)

Goal: handle all routine situations, narrate past and future, express needs and opinions simply. All 7 cases in active use by exit; aspect awareness begins.

| # | Unit (theme) | Vocab domains | Lesson-by-lesson grammar allocation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Na putu** — travel | transport, tickets, hotel, border, luggage | L1 **future I** (ću/ćeš + infinitive; pisat ću spelling) · L2 future I questions/negation (hoću li, neću) · L3 GEN prepositions (iz, do, od, kod, bez, blizu) · L4 travel phrases + putovati/letjeti · L5 planning a trip, review |
| 10 | **Kod kuće** — home & living | rooms, furniture, chores, renting | L1 **DAT singular** (giving/telling: dajem mami) · L2 DAT prepositions (prema, k) + sviđati se intro · L3 household verbs + ACC/DAT contrast · L4 living situations (stanovati, iznajmiti) · L5 my home, review |
| 11 | **Zdravlje** — health & body | body parts, symptoms, doctor, pharmacy | L1 boli me + NOM (glava me boli pattern) · L2 **modals**: morati, moći, smjeti, trebati + infinitive · L3 DAT experiencer constructions (loše mi je, treba mi) · L4 at the doctor's · L5 healthy habits, review |
| 12 | **Posao** — work & study | jobs, office, school subjects, applications | L1 **INS singular** (s/sa + company: s kolegom) · L2 INS of means (no preposition: vlakom, autom) · L3 raditi kao + professions declined · L4 school/university vocab + studirati · L5 job interview basics, review |
| 13 | **Priroda** — nature & seasons | landscape, animals, seasons, weather (extended) | L1 **GEN plural** (-a, -i; fleeting a: pasa, mačaka) · L2 quantities + GEN pl (puno ljudi, pet kuna) · L3 **comparatives** (-iji, -ši; bolji/gori/veći/manji) · L4 superlatives (naj-) + comparisons (od + GEN, nego) · L5 describing places, review |
| 14 | **Proslave** — celebrations & food culture | holidays, birthdays, recipes, gifts | L1 **VOC** (Ivane! Ana! gospodine!) · L2 **imperative** (formation: -j/-i/-ite) · L3 negative imperative (nemoj + inf) + recipes · L4 dates (GEN: prvog siječnja) + months review · L5 invitations & wishes (čestitam ti + DAT), review |
| 15 | **Komunikacija** — phone, mail, internet | phone, email, social media, messages | L1 **object clitic pronouns** ACC (ga, je, ih, me, te) · L2 DAT clitics (mi, ti, mu, joj, nam) · L3 clitic placement (second position; basic cluster aux+DAT+ACC) · L4 phone/email phrases + writing a short message · L5 review + integrated practice |
| 16 | **Moja priča** — biography & plans | life events, CV-style vocab, dreams/plans | L1 **verb aspect introduced** (imperfective/perfective pairs as vocabulary: pisati/napisati, čitati/pročitati) · L2 aspect in past (process vs completion) · L3 aspect in future (radit ću vs napravit ću) · L4 life-story narration (roditi se, odrasti, završiti) · L5 my story + plans, review |

**→ A2 Checkpoint Exam** (`curriculum/A2/checkpoint-exam.md`): same 4-skill format + a short guided writing task (40–60 words, AI-graded). Pass ≥ 70%.

## B1 — Fluency Foundations (Units 17–24)

Goal: discuss opinions, hypotheticals, and abstract-ish topics; read adapted texts comfortably; write 80–150-word compositions. Aspect becomes reliable; clitic cluster fully automatic.

| # | Unit | Theme | Core grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17 | Mediji | media & news basics | Aspect mastery I: pair-formation patterns (prefixation, -nu-, suppletive pairs) |
| 18 | Obrazovanje | education & learning | Conditional I (bih/bi + participle): polite requests, hypotheticals |
| 19 | Mišljenja | opinions & debate | Relative clauses: **koji declined through all cases** |
| 20 | Okoliš | environment | se-passive & impersonal constructions (govori se, može se) |
| 21 | Tehnologija | technology & internet | Indirect speech (da-clauses, reported questions, tense logic) |
| 22 | Kultura i povijest | Croatian culture & history | Future II (budem + participle) in temporal/conditional clauses; kad/ako/čim |
| 23 | Osjećaji | emotions & relationships | Full clitic-cluster ordering (li–aux–DAT–ACC/GEN–se–je); verbal nouns (-nje) |
| 24 | Planovi | plans, careers, decisions | Word formation: diminutives, prefixes, adjective→adverb; all-case plural consolidation with adjectives |

Each B1 unit: 5 lessons + 300–500-word adapted story + unit test + **one guided writing task (80–150 words, AI rubric-graded)**. Lesson-level blueprints live in `curriculum/B1/units.md` (fleshed to full specs as we approach Phase 4).

**→ B1 Checkpoint Exam**: 4 skills + essay section.

## B2 — Proficiency (Units 25–32)

Goal: follow native media, argue a position, write structured essays, handle register. Content shifts weight: fewer teach slides, more authentic-style reading, AI debates, essays.

| # | Unit | Theme | Core grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | Vijesti i politika | news & politics | Nuanced aspect: iterativity, phase verbs (početi/prestati + inf/imperfective) |
| 26 | Rad i ekonomija | work & economy | Conditional II (bio bih + participle); complex conditionals |
| 27 | Znanost | science & research | Passive participles (-n/-t) and passive voice; nominal style |
| 28 | Književnost | literature & storytelling | Verbal adverbs (radeći, uradivši); aorist/imperfect **recognition only** |
| 29 | Argumentacija | argumentation & rhetoric | Complex subordination (iako, premda, budući da, kako bi, umjesto da) |
| 30 | Registar | register & formality | ti/Vi mastery, formal correspondence, slang awareness |
| 31 | Humor i idiomi | humor, idioms, collocations | Idioms & collocations; wordplay; false friends |
| 32 | Hrvatska danas | regional & contemporary Croatia | Regional awareness (ijekavian/ekavian, Zagreb/Dalmatian flavor — receptive); course capstone |

Each B2 unit: 5 lessons + 500–800-word authentic-register text + unit test + **essay (200–300 words)** + **AI tutor debate/role-play session**. Lesson-level blueprints in `curriculum/B2/units.md`.

**→ B2 Final Exam**: full 4-skill exam + essay + recorded speaking tasks; modeled loosely on B2 certificate exams.

## Milestones (what Noah can DO)

- **End of A1 (~week 10):** hold a basic conversation, order in a restaurant, read a 150-word story, talk about yesterday.
- **End of A2 (~month 6):** travel independently in Croatia, describe symptoms to a doctor, use all 7 cases, narrate past/future, first written paragraphs.
- **End of B1 (~month 10):** debate everyday topics with the AI tutor, read adapted literature, write structured paragraphs, aspect mostly automatic.
- **End of B2 (~month 14):** follow Croatian news, write essays, argue positions, understand regional variation receptively.

## Build/authoring order

Content is built strictly in course order (A1 U1 → … → B2 U32) via the pipeline in `01-teaching-method.md` §Content pipeline. The app is always usable with however much content exists — the path ends at the last built unit.
